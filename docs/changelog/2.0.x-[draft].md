# 2.0.x — Serrajin

**Status:** draft  
**Since release:** 2.0.35 (`v2.0.35`)

User-facing changes for the next **2.0.x** patch. Update on every push; edit or remove bullets when later pushes correct earlier work. On ship, this file becomes `done/X.Y.Z-[released].md` (exact patch from the release tag) and a fresh `2.0.x-[draft].md` starts.

Use the [thematic groups](../../.cursor/rules/changelog.mdc#thematic-groups-forja) below — **add a `### Group` heading only when that group has bullets**; never leave empty group headers. Every bullet starts with `**Add:**` · `**Change:**` · `**Fix:**` · `**Remove:**`.

---

### Player

- **Fix:** On desktop, the mouse pointer hides with the player controls when idle and comes back when you move the mouse.
- **Fix:** On desktop, Up and Down change volume only while the volume button is hovered or selected.
- **Fix:** When the connection drops mid-episode, playback reconnects where you were instead of restarting from the beginning or jumping to the end.
- **Fix:** Retry after a failed stream picks up from where you were, and a Forja stream that died after a reconnect looks for a fresh link instead of stopping.
- **Fix:** The audio menu names each track by its language on streams with several audio tracks, instead of Audio 1, Audio 2, and the preferred audio language picks the right one.
- **Fix:** Live Sports on Mac reconnects when the hardware decoder stops producing frames, instead of stuttering and stopping a few seconds in while the audio clock keeps running.
- **Fix:** Live Sports on Windows uses software decode, so the stream no longer freezes the app and sticks on the last frame.

### UI
- **Fix:** On Windows, icons no longer show as empty boxes on some PCs — Forja ships the full icon font.
- **Fix:** On Windows and Linux, poster and card covers on Home, Anime, Asian Drama and other catalog rows load again instead of staying dark.
- **Fix:** Clicking the My List pin on a poster card opens the list menu without also opening the title's details.

- **Fix:** A hub that showed “Couldn’t reach the catalog” while offline loads again when you open it after the connection returns, instead of keeping the error.

### Sources

- **Fix:** Cancelling the loading page or closing the player stops the torrent, instead of leaving it downloading in the background.
- **Add:** Settings → Addons → Direct torrent lists the torrents in the engine with progress, peers and speed, and lets you stop one or all of them.
- **Add:** VoirAnime and Anime-Sama anime sources, with French dub (VF) and French-subtitled (VOSTFR) streams. VF streams show when the Anime hub is set to DUB.
- **Fix:** StreamFlix matches the right title and lists only streams that open.
- **Fix:** MoviesDrive finds movies again, and its Google Drive, 10Gbps and PixelServer streams play.
- **Fix:** MoviesMod **Driveseed Instant** streams play again, and UHDMovies lists its Instant streams again.
- **Fix:** Castle quality labels match the stream that plays.

### Downloads

- **Fix:** Direct-file downloads such as MoviesDrive save a playable video.
- **Fix:** A download keeps the subtitles its source lists, such as Castle's, and they show in the subtitle menu when you play the saved file.
- **Fix:** Hubs already on the device, such as Downloads, open without internet instead of waiting on a loading screen.

### Live & IPTV

- **Fix:** On desktop, the loading stripes on a portal card stay inside the card while its channels load, instead of spilling over the rows around it.
- **Fix:** Switching portals in IPTV loads the new portal's channels, movies and series right away, instead of showing "No channels" or "No movies" until you reload the hub.

### TV

- **Add:** Packs can mark a plugin for desktop or phone only. On Android TV such a plugin stays off, Forja Packs shows it as **Not available on this device**, and the Official packs picker tags it **Not on this device**, while the same profile keeps it on your other devices.
- **Fix:** In the Official packs picker, clicking a pack's checkbox with a mouse or touch selects it, instead of flipping back to unselected.
