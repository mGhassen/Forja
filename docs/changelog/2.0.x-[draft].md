# 2.0.x — Serrajin

User-facing changes for the next **2.0.x** patch. Update on every push; edit or remove bullets when later pushes correct earlier work. On ship, this file becomes `done/X.Y.Z-[released].md` (exact patch from the release tag) and a fresh `2.0.x-[draft].md` starts.

Use the [thematic groups](../../.cursor/rules/changelog.mdc#thematic-groups-forja) below — **add a `### Group` heading only when that group has bullets**; never leave empty group headers. Every bullet starts with `**Add:**` · `**Change:**` · `**Fix:**` · `**Remove:**`.

### Live & IPTV

- **Fix:** IPTV Live **EPG** switches the channel pane to the programme guide again (Cards ↔ EPG).
- **Fix:** BZH-Sport lists player mirrors again and unlocks gated CDNs on play (plus Daddy host recognition).
- **Fix:** Anime details loading screen shows the series name, not “4th Season” from the cour you opened.
- **Fix:** Anime episode cards get TMDB stills again after enrich (matched to the selected Season / OVA), without inventing extra episodes.
- **Fix:** Anime Sources footer follows the season chip label (S02 / OVA01 / SPECIAL01), not the rail index or media-local S01.
- **Fix:** Switching Season / OVA chips on anime details updates in place (no full-page reload flash).
- **Fix:** Anime season chips keep TBA / untitled sequels after aired cours and honor “2nd Season” titles (no fake Season 3).
- **Fix:** Anime season switch drops the previous cour’s logo/backdrops so TMDB enrich stays on that Media only.
- **Fix:** Anime Season 1 episode titles match that season (no franchise “Episode 49+” leftovers from later cours).
- **Fix:** Anime later-season chips get that season’s episode titles and stills (not Season 1 leftovers).

### Sources

- **Fix:** Opening Sources shows installed Forja / Torrents / Stremio / Nuvio provider chips immediately (no empty wait while prefs catch up).
- **Fix:** Anime / Asian Drama Torrents search uses a short series name plus `SxxExx` (season chip) instead of the full cour title with only `01`, so Nyaa and similar indexers return hits again.

### Lists

- **Add:** Search on My List filters titles in the current status and kind, with hit counts on status tabs and kind chips.
- **Fix:** After you switch Forja accounts or profiles on this device, My List shows only that account’s titles.
- **Fix:** Anime season chips refresh My List pin per season (watching / completed no longer stuck on the previous cour).
- **Fix:** Pinning one anime season no longer deletes your My List status on the other seasons.

### Player

- **Fix:** ExoPlayer seek bar shows IntroDB intro/credits (and next-episode) color zones like MediaKit.
- **Fix:** Online subtitles (Wyzie and similar) paint on Android TV Exo after pick — file type follows the download body, not a guess from the URL.
- **Fix:** MediaKit on phone/TV shows loading when you pick an online subtitle (same as Exo).
- **Fix:** ExoPlayer In-stream (HLS playlist) subtitles paint after pick — same as MediaKit.
- **Fix:** In-player Settings scrolls when Aspect ratio / Loop sit below the fold on tall screens.
- **Fix:** On Android TV, pressing Back while playing shows **Press Back again to exit** before leaving the player.
- **Fix:** On Android TV, when **Skip Intro**, **Skip Recap**, **Skip Credits**, or **Next Episode** appears, the remote lands on that button.
- **Fix:** MediaKit anime / HLS on Android TV (and desktop) rebuilds a deeper buffer before resuming after a hitch (less freeze-every-few-seconds vs Exo).
- **Fix:** MegaPlay / AnimeKai PNG-wrapped HLS keeps strip prefetch ahead of the playhead (and macOS uses GPU decode) to cut freeze-then-jump / lipsync hits.
- **Fix:** macOS MediaKit **HW+** uses the hardware video texture again (no forced soft display) with correct colors after OpenGL↔BGRA alignment.
- **Add:** Auto picture-in-picture in the Android phone app: leave the app while a video plays and it keeps playing in a small window. On by default; turn it off in Settings → Addons → Playback. Each phone keeps its own choice.
- **Change:** The phone player has a simpler control bar: time, Next episode, the source, Episodes, Audio & subtitles, and More. More holds Quality, Settings, Volume, Previous episode, and Lock on MediaKit. Double-tap the left or right side to skip 10 s, with a bubble that shows the total. Menus open below the top bar, and leaving the player returns to portrait with the status bar showing.
- **Fix:** On phone, the player source chip uses touch density — it ellipsizes when there is room and becomes icon-only when the bar is tight, instead of painting a yellow overflow stripe.
- **Fix:** Opening a stream on phone no longer kills the app — MediaKit keeps a smaller buffer while software decode is on.

### UI

- **Fix:** Switching back to a hub opens at the top of the page again (kept paint, reset scroll).
- **Fix:** Opening title details no longer freezes while every trailer warms YouTube in the background.
- **Change:** Sidebar icons stay small when a tab is selected but focus is elsewhere; they enlarge only on focus or hover. Profile name turns green on focus.
- **Fix:** Nuvio addon enable switch flips immediately in Settings (no leave/reenter).
- **Fix:** Stremio addon **Sources** / **Live Sports** chips respond to clicks in Settings again.
- **Change:** In the Android phone app, phones keep the touch layout and the bottom tab bar in landscape and when you rotate, and tablets get the side rail. The tab bar shows up to five tabs; the rest, Settings included, open from **More**. In landscape the bar is shorter, with each icon beside its label, and it always clears the gesture bar.
- **Change:** Phone bottom tabs use the same accent colors, underline, and hover grow as the desktop/TV sidebar — no grey pill wash.
- **Change:** Phone bottom tabs match the side rail: each tab’s accent color, an underline when selected, and hover/press icon scale — no Material pill wash.
- **Fix:** In the Android phone app, the status bar stays visible and content stays below the clock. Hubs show Search and their menus in a top bar that stays readable over bright art and turns solid as you scroll. Notifications and the update banner show above the tab bar.
- **Change:** In the Android phone app, details show a full-width Play button that names the episode, such as Resume S1E3, with labeled Sources, Trailer, My List and SUB/DUB buttons underneath. Episodes are a vertical list with thumbnail, date, length, progress and a play button. The back button is larger, a title bar appears once you scroll, and landscape keeps genres, rating and synopsis.
- **Change:** In the Android phone app, catalogs show about three posters across in portrait and five to six in landscape. Long-press a poster for Play, Details or My List. The home hero runs edge to edge with a Play button, crossfades between slides, uses horizontal dots, and keeps its title and buttons above the tab bar in landscape. The selected mood stands out more.
- **Change:** In the Android phone app, details Sources with its Filters and hub Categories open as bottom sheets. Settings stays one list in landscape, My List status tabs scroll sideways with full names, and Search in landscape fits with the keyboard open. Live Sports loading shows as a thin bar under the action bar, and Live Sports and IPTV action bars scroll sideways on narrow screens.
- **Change:** Home shows Continue Watching right after the hero.
- **Fix:** Details show either the title text or the logo while the logo loads, never both on top of each other.

### TV

- **Fix:** On series details, D-pad ↓ from a season returns to the last focused episode (and ↑ keeps the last season), same as other rails.
- **Fix:** Android TV no longer freezes every few seconds while LAN is paired to a desktop — the nav no longer hammers desktop health checks in the background.
- **Change:** Turning off the LAN addon also clears pairing (TV/phone must pair again; desktop revokes paired devices).

### Features

- **Change:** On phone, Profile & account uses Web login only — no email/password fields in the app.
- **Fix:** Web login recovers from a flaky account-service lookup — the app warms DNS, retries apply, and the portal retries Return to Forja so you usually do not need a second tap.
- **Fix:** Android phone / TV keeps you signed in after a cold start (session survives EncryptedSharedPreferences / prefs flaking).
- **Fix:** Website GitHub links open the Forja repo (`mGhassen/Forja`), not the old forjahq path.
- **Fix:** Signup email confirm opens profile setup instead of the marketing home.
- **Add:** Spanish anime providers (subtitles + Latino/Castellano dub).
- **Change:** DUB language chips split Spanish variants — Castellano, Latino, and Catalán.
- **Add:** Android phone app for phones and tablets, with its own Android download on the website next to Android TV. Phones running the Android TV app move to it with the next update and keep their library and settings.
