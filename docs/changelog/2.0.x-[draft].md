# 2.0.x — Serrajin

**Status:** draft  
**Since release:** 2.0.35 (`v2.0.35`)

User-facing changes for the next **2.0.x** patch. Update on every push; edit or remove bullets when later pushes correct earlier work. On ship, this file becomes `done/X.Y.Z-[released].md` (exact patch from the release tag) and a fresh `2.0.x-[draft].md` starts.

Use the [thematic groups](../../.cursor/rules/changelog.mdc#thematic-groups-forja) below — **add a `### Group` heading only when that group has bullets**; never leave empty group headers. Every bullet starts with `**Add:**` · `**Change:**` · `**Fix:**` · `**Remove:**`.

### Features

- **Fix:** Anime keeps loading when AniList is down — it falls back to a backup catalog mirror.
- **Fix:** The forjahq.xyz website and account portal load again instead of showing a server error on every page.
- **Fix:** Home **New Releases** stays filled when you pick a watch service (for example Crunchyroll) instead of showing one poster or an empty row.
- **Change:** The Aflem tab shows the whole Brstej catalog: latest episodes, most viewed, a row for each section (series by region, Ramadan back to 2021, films, TV shows), and every series at the bottom. Rows keep loading as you scroll. Films, Series, and Categories narrow the tab to matching rows.
- **Fix:** In Aflem, a show opened from one of its episodes lists all its episodes again, and search no longer adds the same eight unrelated shows to every result.
- **Change:** The Arabic tab shows the whole Larozaa catalog: most viewed in the hero, every section row with all its titles, and every series at the bottom. Rows keep loading as you scroll, film and show names are clean, and search puts the closest title first.
- **Fix:** Arabic Turkish series load again, and shows open with their real name, synopsis, and every season. Films opened from Continue Watching open as films.
- **Change:** كرتون and Kids show their whole catalogs: every row keeps loading as you scroll, a new **كل المسلسلات (أ - ي)** row lists every series A–Z, and the letter filter covers all titles instead of the first page.
- **Fix:** Kids shows open with every season, also from Continue Watching, without repeated episodes, and film names drop the "فيلم" prefix. كرتون's letter filter matches titles that start with أ or إ.

### Player

- **Fix:** On desktop, Video decode **SW** now uses software display as well as software decode — pink/green wrong colors from the hardware texture path clear when you pick SW.
- **Fix:** Finishing an episode keeps the show on Continue Watching as the next episode (or next season) instead of removing it — only the last episode of a show drops off.
- **Fix:** Player controls hide when you leave the app, show again when you come back, and only idle-hide after you move the mouse or press a key — not while you are still moving.
- **Fix:** Scrubbing or skipping on MediaKit (Mac / desktop / phone) resumes sooner on network streams — seeks snap to a keyframe instead of waiting through a long accurate decode.
- **Change:** On Mac and Windows, picture-in-picture opens a separate floating player while Forja stays full-size — you can keep browsing, with a **Playing in picture-in-picture** notice and **Expand** to return.
- **Add:** AirPlay on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Tap **Cast**, pick an Apple TV or AirPlay TV, and the video plays there with the subtitle you picked in Forja. Switch back to your device and Forja picks up where the TV stopped.
- **Add:** Live Sports has **Cast** (AirPlay on iPhone, iPad, and Mac) in the player top bar — same as movies.
- **Fix:** AirPlay works for offline downloads on the same Wi-Fi — the TV plays the saved file instead of failing to open.
- **Fix:** While AirPlay is playing, the seek bar in Forja keeps up with the TV, scrubbing seeks on the TV, and play/pause in Forja controls the TV.
- **Fix:** While AirPlay is playing, Forja shows **Playing on AirPlay** and the cast button stays on until you stop casting.
- **Fix:** Next episode (and other in-player switches) keep AirPlay on the TV — you do not have to cast again.
- **Fix:** While AirPlay is playing, the app no longer sticks on **Buffering…** — that was the local player, not the TV.
- **Fix:** While AirPlay is playing, **Skip Intro**, **Skip Credits**, and **Next Episode** still show and work from the TV playhead.
- **Fix:** AirPlay shows the stream’s own (in-stream) subtitles on the TV for HLS, not only online or addon subtitle files.
- **Fix:** AirPlay keeps playing when the Mac goes idle — casting no longer stops after the display sleep timer.
- **Add:** On desktop, the Subtitles menu has a **File** button next to **Off** to load a subtitle file from your computer (SRT, VTT, ASS, SSA).
- **Fix:** Subtitles no longer show the same line twice on top of each other.
- **Fix:** On desktop, the mouse pointer hides with the player controls when idle and comes back when you move the mouse.
- **Fix:** On desktop, Up and Down change volume only while the volume button is hovered or selected.
- **Fix:** When the connection drops mid-episode, playback reconnects where you were instead of restarting from the beginning or jumping to the end.
- **Fix:** Retry after a failed stream picks up from where you were, and a Forja stream that died after a reconnect looks for a fresh link instead of stopping.
- **Fix:** The audio menu names each track by its language on streams with several audio tracks, instead of Audio 1, Audio 2, and the preferred audio language picks the right one.
- **Fix:** Live Sports on Mac reconnects when the hardware decoder stops producing frames, instead of stuttering and stopping a few seconds in while the audio clock keeps running.
- **Fix:** Live Sports on Windows uses software decode, so the stream no longer freezes the app and sticks on the last frame.
- **Fix:** With Auto subtitles off, the player keeps the stream's subtitle or the one you pick and does not load online subtitles on its own.
- **Fix:** Online subtitles no longer show a "free plan" ad line at the start of an episode.
- **Fix:** The subtitle menu lists the stream's own subtitles as soon as playback starts, including after a resume or a quality change.
- **Fix:** Offline playback no longer shows a fake **Unknown** subtitle folder with **Audio auto** / **Audio no** — those media_kit placeholders stay out of the list.
- **Fix:** Next Episode no longer shows when the next episode has not aired yet, or on the last episode of a show.
- **Change:** Skip Intro, Skip Recap, Skip Credits, Skip Preview and Next Episode stay on screen while the player controls are hidden, and the buttons are larger. On TV, the remote lands on them so OK skips.
- **Change:** Play reaches the provider race sooner: provider scripts are read from disk once per session instead of on every Play, and providers without a linked account no longer probe the secure store.
- **Change:** Video opens as soon as a stream is verified; subtitle tracks that are still downloading attach once the picture is ready instead of holding it, and never replace a subtitle you picked yourself.
- **Change:** While a stream opens, the player shows a centered indicator with the source being checked until the first frame appears.
- **Add:** When no source plays, the player shows a centered "Failed to stream" message with Retry and Back that the remote can reach; Retry takes focus right away.
- **Add:** On TV, the stream-resolve screen says that the remote Back button cancels.
- **Fix:** Players open http(s) streams, the local proxy, and files in the Downloads folder; other addresses show "Unsupported stream address" instead of being handed to the system.
- **Change:** Forja's local playback relay answers the Forja player alone; other apps and browser pages on the device cannot use it.
- **Fix:** Release builds do not print stream addresses, portal credentials, headers, or sign-in links to the device log, and Android does not back up app settings or installed packs to the cloud.
- **Fix:** When the first server dies, the player moves on to the other servers that answered during the race, then searches again if they all fail, instead of stopping at "Failed to stream".
- **Fix:** Picking an online subtitle on Android and Android TV turns it on. The player downloads it, shows it while it loads, switches to that exact subtitle even when another one in the same language is already on, and the menu marks it as selected. If the file cannot be attached, the player says so instead of leaving the row checked with no text.
- **Fix:** The subtitle menu lists each attached subtitle once instead of repeating it as an in-stream track, and the list does not grow when the player retries a stream or when you move to the next episode.
- **Fix:** Switching source in the player keeps the subtitle you picked, and a subtitle you pick wins over one the player was loading on its own.
