### Live & IPTV

- **Fix:** IPTV Live **EPG** switches the channel pane to the programme guide again (Cards ↔ EPG).
- **Fix:** BZH-Sport lists player mirrors again and unlocks gated CDNs on play (plus Daddy host recognition).
- **Fix:** Anime details loading screen shows the series name, not “4th Season” from the cour you opened.
- **Fix:** Anime episode cards get TMDB stills again after enrich (matched to the selected Season / OVA), without inventing extra episodes.
- **Fix:** Anime Sources footer follows the season chip label (S02 / OVA01 / SPECIAL01), not the rail index or media-local S01.
- **Fix:** Switching Season / OVA chips on anime details updates in place (no full-page reload flash).
- **Fix:** Anime season chips keep TBA / untitled sequels after aired cours and honor “2nd Season” titles (no fake Season 3).
- **Fix:** Anime season switch drops the previous cour’s logo/backdrops so TMDB enrich stays on that Media only.
- **Fix:** Anime Season 1 episode titles match that season (no franchise “Episode 49+” leftovers from later cours).
- **Fix:** Anime later-season chips get that season’s episode titles and stills (not Season 1 leftovers).

### Lists

- **Add:** Search on My List filters titles in the current status and kind, with hit counts on status tabs and kind chips.
- **Fix:** After you switch Forja accounts or profiles on this device, My List shows only that account’s titles.
- **Fix:** Anime season chips refresh My List pin per season (watching / completed no longer stuck on the previous cour).
- **Fix:** Pinning one anime season no longer deletes your My List status on the other seasons.

### Player

- **Fix:** Online subtitles (Wyzie and similar) paint on Android TV Exo after pick — file type follows the download body, not a guess from the URL.
- **Fix:** MediaKit on phone/TV shows loading when you pick an online subtitle (same as Exo).
- **Fix:** ExoPlayer In-stream (HLS playlist) subtitles paint after pick — same as MediaKit.
- **Fix:** In-player Settings scrolls when Aspect ratio / Loop sit below the fold on tall screens.
- **Fix:** On Android TV, pressing Back while playing shows **Press Back again to exit** before leaving the player.
- **Fix:** On Android TV, when **Skip Intro**, **Skip Recap**, **Skip Credits**, or **Next Episode** appears, the remote lands on that button.
- **Fix:** MediaKit anime / HLS on Android TV (and desktop) rebuilds a deeper buffer before resuming after a hitch (less freeze-every-few-seconds vs Exo).
- **Fix:** MegaPlay / AnimeKai PNG-wrapped HLS keeps strip prefetch ahead of the playhead (and macOS uses GPU decode) to cut freeze-then-jump / lipsync hits.
- **Fix:** macOS MediaKit **HW+** uses the hardware video texture again (no forced soft display) with correct colors after OpenGL↔BGRA alignment.

### UI

- **Change:** Sidebar icons stay small when a tab is selected but focus is elsewhere; they enlarge only on focus or hover. Profile name turns green on focus.
- **Fix:** Nuvio addon enable switch flips immediately in Settings (no leave/reenter).
- **Fix:** Stremio addon **Sources** / **Live Sports** chips respond to clicks in Settings again.

### TV

- **Fix:** On series details, D-pad ↓ from a season returns to the last focused episode (and ↑ keeps the last season), same as other rails.
