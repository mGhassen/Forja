### Player
- **Add:** Content warnings overlay at the start of a movie or episode (nudity, violence, and other IMDb parents-guide ratings). Turn it off in Settings → Playback or the in-player Settings menu.
- **Fix:** Left nav rail stays hidden while any fullscreen player is open (still kept mounted so exit does not flash a remount).

### Live & IPTV
- **Change:** Live Matches embed drops the black title bar after load — full-height video with floating Back / Mute / source badge on top.

### UI
- **Fix:** Search result cards show FILM or TV next to the year, same as Home.
- **Change:** Trakt in Settings → Connected services is admin-only. Everyone else sees Simkl and MDBlist.
- **Change:** My List is a poster grid. Top menu is Films / TV Shows / Anime (same as Home; tap again for all). Status row is Plan to Watch / Watching / On Hold / Completed / Dropped for local and Simkl. Details **+** always opens those statuses (writes local; Simkl when connected). Year and rating sit under the title.
- **Fix:** Opening a Trakt or MDBlist list from Settings no longer shows a transparent page — solid background and poster grid.
- **Remove:** Download button on the details hero (Sources still opens from the white Play / Resume).
- **Change:** Details hero icon pills (My List / Simkl statuses) expand with a label on hover, same as Play.
- **Change:** Home poster bookmark opens Plan to Watch / Watching / On Hold / Completed / Dropped (icons + labels), not a plain add/remove.
- **Fix:** My List posters match Home card size, stay in their grid on hover, and no longer cover the status tabs.

### Features
- **Fix:** Simkl sync pulls completed movies, TV, and anime history (not only plan-to-watch), and in-progress titles into continue watching. List buckets also mirror onto local My List.
- **Fix:** Trashing resume progress on details also clears that title from Simkl Completed.
- **Change:** On desktop, hover Home in the nav for ~1s to open the streaming-service panel (was ~500ms).
