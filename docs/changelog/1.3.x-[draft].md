### Player

- **Fix:** On Android TV the MediaKit engine no longer sounds much quieter than ExoPlayer. Switching engines used to mean reaching for the TV remote to fix the level; both now play at a comparable loudness at the same volume.

### Live & IPTV

- **Fix:** Live channels no longer restart themselves while the stream is already recovering. When an IPTV server briefly drops the connection the player used to tear everything down and show "Reconnecting…", even though the underlying recovery was seconds from finishing on its own. It now lets that finish and only reconnects if the stream really is gone.
