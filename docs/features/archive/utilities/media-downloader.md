# Media Downloader

> **Archived:** Out of scope or not in the default navigation surface. See [Archive](../README.md).

> Find direct download links for TV episodes from the 111477 index.

## What it is

Media Downloader searches TMDB for a show, lets you pick season and episode, then queries the 111477.xyz file index for direct download URLs. Useful when you want files to keep offline rather than stream.

## How to open it

The **Media Downloader** tab is temporarily hidden from the navigation bar and Settings → Features.

## What you can do

- Search TMDB for a series
- Select season and episode
- View matched download links from the index
- Open or copy links (platform-dependent)

## Tips

- Availability depends on what's indexed on 111477 — not every episode will have hits
- This is separate from torrent search on [Media details](../../movies-tv/tmdb-details.md)
