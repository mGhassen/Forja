# Discover

> **Archived:** Out of scope or not in the default navigation surface. See [Archive](../README.md).

> Browse TMDB with filters — movies, TV, genres, years, ratings, and language.

## What it is

Discover is a filterable catalog browser. Pick movies, TV shows, or both, then narrow by genre, release year, minimum rating, and language. Results load with infinite scroll so you can keep browsing without pagination taps.

## How to open it

The **Discover** tab is temporarily hidden from the navigation bar and Settings → Features.

## What you can do

- Switch between Movies, TV, and All
- Filter by genre, year range, minimum rating, and language
- Scroll to load more results
- Tap any title to open details and play

## Tips

- Combine filters to find niche content (e.g. French dramas from the 1990s)
- Discover uses TMDB metadata — playback still goes through torrents, streaming mode, or addons on the details screen
