# Manga

> Search, read, and track manga chapters.

## What it is

Browse and search a manga catalog, filter by genre, paginate results, like series, and continue where you left off. Open a series for chapter list and read page-by-page or in continuous scroll.

## How to open it

The **Manga** tab is temporarily hidden from the navigation bar and Settings → Navigation.

## What you can do

- Search and filter by genre
- Like manga to your list
- Resume from reading history
- Open chapter reader (page or scroll mode)
- Navigate pages and chapters

## Tips

- Reading progress is saved per series
