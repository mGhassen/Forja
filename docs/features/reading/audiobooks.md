# Audiobooks

> Browse, play, and download audiobooks with chapter navigation.

## What it is

Search and browse audiobooks from multiple sources in parallel. Like titles, track listening history, play with chapter navigation, speed control, and resume. Download chapters for offline listening.

## How to open it

Tap **Audiobooks** in the navigation bar.

## What you can do

- Search and browse catalog from multiple sources (GoldenAudiobook, Audiozaic, AppAudiobooks, Audionest, ZAudiobooks, FullLength, HDAudiobooks, BigAudiobooks, and Tokybook when available)
- Like audiobooks
- Resume from listening history
- Play in the audiobook player (chapters, speed, seek) — progress bar and thumb use Forja brand green
- Download chapters for offline
- Open downloads manager for saved chapters

## Tips

- Playback speed is independent from video [playback speed](../playback/playback-speed.md)
- Multiple scrapers run in parallel for faster search results
