# Comics

> Search, read, and track comic series page by page.

## What it is

Search comics, maintain a liked list, and resume reading history. The reader loads pages through a local comic proxy for reliable image delivery. A secondary source (ReadComicsOnline) supplements the primary catalog.

## How to open it

The **Comics** tab is temporarily hidden from the navigation bar and Settings → Features.

## What you can do

- Search comics
- Like series for quick access
- Continue from reading history
- Read chapters in the page reader
- View comic details and chapter list

## Tips

- Some series may come from alternate sources automatically when matched
