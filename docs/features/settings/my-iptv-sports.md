# My IPTV sports

> Play Live Matches cards using channels on your Xtream portal.

## What it is

On-device Sports matcher (Sportio-style): the **same** Live Matches schedule as **All** (PPV · Streamed · CDN), then name/EPG matching against live channels on **one** Xtream portal. Streams play in the native IPTV player. No self-hosted addon. Not an ESPN-only grid.

## How to open it

- **Live Matches → Servers → My IPTV** (portal via top-right **Portals**)
- **Settings → My IPTV sports** for Enable / optional folder narrowing

## What you can do

- Browse the same sports catalog as All
- Pick the Xtream portal from top-right **Portals** (same IPTV panel)
- Open a match → sheet of ranked matching IPTV channels → pick one to play (failover keeps the rest in the player Source menu)
- Optionally narrow live folders by sport family in Settings

## Setup

1. Add an Xtream portal under IPTV (if you don’t have one)
2. Live Matches → Servers → **My IPTV** → top-right **Portals** if needed
3. Optional: Settings → My IPTV sports for folder narrowing

## Tips

- Catalog = existing Live Matches sources — not ESPN
- Portal is only in Live Matches / IPTV Portals (not Settings)
- Empty folders = search all live channels on that portal
- No match toast means the portal had no channel name/EPG hit for that game
