# Wire Forja Sports to IPTV

> Match live fixtures to your portal channels on the Live TV tab.

## Steps

1. Connect at least one IPTV portal.
2. Open **Settings → Addons → Live Sports** (or hub **Setup**).
3. Enable **Forja Sports** / Live TV matching as exposed in Setup.
4. On Live Sports, pick the portal under **Portals** (same active portal as IPTV).
5. Open a match → **Live TV** for portal channel matches.
