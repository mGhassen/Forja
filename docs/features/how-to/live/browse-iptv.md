# Browse IPTV live, movies, and series

> Watch channels and VOD from your active portal.

## Steps

1. Open the **IPTV** tab.
2. Pick **Live**, **Movies**, or **Series** from the shelf.
3. Use **Portals** to select the active portal if you have more than one.
4. On Live: pick a category, then a channel card. Movies / Series open hub details.
5. Play in the native IPTV player.
