# Filter Home by Films, TV, or Categories

> Limit Home rails to movies, series, or a TMDB genre.

## Steps

1. Open **Home**.
2. In the hero menu, pick **Films**, **TV Shows**, or **Categories**.
3. **Categories** lists TMDB genres — picking one refilters hero, Featured, Popular, mood results, and most rails.
4. Pick **All** (or clear) to mix films and series again.

## Tips

- The three rotating genre rows at the bottom keep their own genres.
- Sparse genres page TMDB until rows fill after dedupe.
