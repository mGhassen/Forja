# Set Green Play preferred providers

> Make the green Play button try your favorite Forja providers first.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings → Addons** (or expand the providers pack under **Forja Packs**).
2. Open **Green Play** / preferred Forja providers and add the ones you want first.
3. On a title, tap green **Play** / **Resume**. Preferred providers race first; with none set, every enabled Forja plugin races. First playable stream wins.

## Tips

- Sources → Forja chips only control the panel list — they do not narrow green Play.
