# Filter Home by streaming service

> Show titles available on a chosen watch provider.

## Steps

1. On **Home**, re-tap **Home** in the nav (you are already there) to open the floating streaming-service panel. On desktop, hover **Home** ~1s also opens it.
2. Tap a service logo. A mark appears before **Films** and rails filter to that provider.
3. Re-open the panel and clear or pick another service to change the filter.
