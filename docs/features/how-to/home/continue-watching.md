# Resume Continue Watching

> Pick up where you left off from the Home rail.

## Steps

1. Open **Home** and find **Continue Watching**.
2. Tap a card to open details with **Resume** (or resume play, depending on the flow).
3. To clear one title: open details → trash icon next to **Resume** (also drops cached extract for that title/episode).
4. To clear the whole rail for this profile: **Settings → Data & backup → Continue watching**.
