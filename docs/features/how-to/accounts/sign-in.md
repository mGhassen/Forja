# Sign in, pick a profile, or continue as guest

> Get into Forja with an account or guest mode.

## Steps

1. On cold start, choose **Sign in** or **Continue as guest**.
2. **Sign in (desktop / TV):** show the code + QR → approve on the portal `/connect` while signed in → pick a profile on **Who’s watching?**.
3. **Guest:** packs step once per device (local); settings stay on this device until you sign in.
4. Later: profile avatar → **Profile & account** to sign in / out or switch profiles.
