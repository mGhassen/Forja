# Link Android TV with a code or QR

> Approve the TV (or desktop) device link on the web portal.

## Steps

1. On the TV (or desktop), choose **Sign in** — note the code under the QR (`XXXX-XXXX`).
2. On phone or computer, open the Forja portal **/connect** (or scan the QR). Sign in if needed.
3. Enter / confirm the code. On desktop, clicking the QR can open `/connect?code=…` and auto-approve after a short countdown.
4. Back on the device: pick a profile, then finish the packs step if prompted.

## Tips

- Codes expire after about 10 minutes — start again if needed.
