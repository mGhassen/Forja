# Jellyfin

> Connect your Jellyfin server and watch your personal media library.

## What it is

Jellyfin integration lets you log into a self-hosted Jellyfin server, browse libraries, resume watching, see Next Up and Latest per library, search, and play movies and series through Forja's player (via a local Jellyfin proxy for authenticated streams).

## How to open it

The **Jellyfin** tab is temporarily hidden from the navigation bar and Settings → Navigation.

## What you can do

- Add server URL and sign in with username/password
- Browse libraries with hero carousel and rails
- Resume in-progress items
- Hover Continue Watching or Next Up cards (desktop) to scale them and show a brand-green play button that floats upward while its play icon pulses slowly
- See Next Up and Latest additions
- Search your library
- Open movie/series details with seasons and episodes
- Play with stream transcoding/direct play as Jellyfin allows

## Setup

1. Run Jellyfin on your network or use a remote URL
2. Enter server address (e.g. `https://jellyfin.example.com`)
3. Log in with your Jellyfin credentials

## Tips

- Server must be reachable from your device (LAN, VPN, or public URL with HTTPS)
- Remote playback quality depends on Jellyfin transcoding settings
