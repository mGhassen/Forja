# IPTV — Xtream

> Connect Xtream Codes portals — live TV, VOD, series, and EPG.

## What it is

The IPTV tab supports **Xtream Codes** portals. The tab opens on the **catalog** (categories + channels). Pick a portal from the side panel, switch **Live / Movies / Series** from the top bar, and play in the IPTV player.

## How to open it

Tap **IPTV** in the navigation bar. If you used a portal before, its catalog loads automatically; otherwise choose a portal from the **Portals** panel.

## What you can do

- Open the **Portals** panel from the top-bar portal control (status dot + portal name; hover/focus expands left to show connection seats `used/max`) — after a portal is active — to add, scrape, search, favorite, or switch providers; until then use **Open portal** in the empty state
- Switch **Live**, **Movies**, and **Series** from section chips in the top bar once a portal is active — the full top bar (shelf, search, portals) is hidden until you pick a provider
- Catalog is **cached on this device** per portal/section (disk + session) — quit and reopen reuses the last fetch for that shelf. Opening a portal (or switching **Live / Movies / Series**) loads **only that shelf**; other shelves load when you open them. **Reload** refreshes the active shelf from the portal. Login, catalogs, and series episodes are fetched and normalized in the Rust engine (Flutter only renders)
- Hover (or focus) the **selected** section chip (**Live** / **Movies** / **Series**) to reveal **Reload** and force a fresh catalog fetch for that shelf (updates the on-device cache)
- Browse live channels, movies, and series by category — tiles and compact list rows show logos or posters fitted inside the card (live logos not cropped to fill), plus a play control that floats upward with a slowly pulsing play icon, and a slightly brighter border, on hover (desktop) or focus (TV). Hover/focus also probes the stream after a short pause and tints the border green (alive) or red (dead) for **Live**, **Movies**, and **Series** (session cache; Series fetches show info and checks the first episode). On **Android TV**, live logos sit in a centered square band above the title (Movies / Series keep poster cover); D-pad **←** from any category row returns to the nav rail, **→** moves to channels, and **↑** from the first category returns to Live / Movies / Series
- On **desktop**, switch Live catalog between **Cards** and **EPG guide** with the pill next to Live / Movies / Series — guide shows a sticky channel column, half-hour time ruler, programme blocks sized by duration (6 hours behind · 24 hours ahead), and a red **Now** line; choice is remembered on this device; Movies / Series / compact keep cards only. On **TV** the EPG channel rail is D-pad focusable (**OK** plays); the favorite pin on a channel is mouse-only
- On **Live**, use the top-bar **Sort** control (between search and portals) to order **Categories** and **Channels** independently: playlist order, name A–Z, or name Z–A — remembered on this device; Movies/Series stay in portal order
- On **Live**, the category sidebar includes **Favorites** and **Already watched** above portal groups — star a channel to favorite it; opening a live channel adds it to Already watched (last 30 on this device, per portal). If the portal returns channels but no category list, the engine synthesizes a **Channels** (and **Uncategorized** when needed) group so Live is not stuck on empty Favorites
- On **Live**, press and hold a portal group row for about **1.5 seconds**, then drag to reorder the category list — the held row lifts as a floating highlighted card while you move it (Favorites / Already watched stay fixed on top). Order is remembered per portal on this device when Sort → Categories is **Playlist Order**. Hover (or TV-focus) also shows a **pin** control that jumps a group to the top of the movable list; Movies / Series have no drag/pin
- **Search** live, movie, and series catalogs (search icon → slide-in bar on wide layouts, dialog on compact layouts; matches channel or group name globally, and the category sidebar lists only groups that contain hits — Favorites / Already watched appear when they have hits). Clearing search keeps the last category you picked or the category of a channel you played; otherwise it restores the category you had before search. After clearing search or returning from a watched channel, the sidebar scrolls to the selected category — on **desktop**, **Cmd+F** / **Ctrl+F** opens the search bar and focuses the field (same as the search icon; macOS **Edit → Find…** too)
- Watch VOD movies and series with seasons/episodes
- View EPG (program guide) when the portal provides it — disable in **Settings → Playback → IPTV programme guide (EPG)** to skip loading guide data
- Favorite portals in the Portals panel — the star is hidden until you hover/focus a row (always visible when already favorited); favorited portals pin to the top with **gold** title text; below them, the most recently scraped, dealt, or added portals appear first (with a **NEW** badge on session-fresh rows until you hover or focus them — styling only, position stays put)
- Portal rows and the top-bar portal control show your **Portal name** when you set one (add / edit / share-code import); otherwise they fall back to the account name from the provider
- Portal rows show the subscription **end date** on the first line (green · orange · red by time left) and **connection seats** (`used/max`) under the server URL (blue when free capacity, gray when full); the **status dot** on the top-bar portal control (and panel rows) probes when a portal becomes active — green/red stay cached about **two minutes**, then the active portal is re-checked automatically; each successful probe also refreshes **expiry** and **used/max** (saved on this device); **Reload** on a shelf also re-checks; hover/focus on idle panel rows still probes when uncached; hover/focus reveals row actions (**copy share code / edit / delete**) and the favorite star (unless already starred) without moving the status icon off-center
- **Copy share code** transforms the row into a share token (copied automatically); click the row again to restore portal details. Normal row click still selects the portal when the code is not shown. New shares are a self-contained `F1.…` token (no pastebin). Old 8-character codes still import when the legacy share host is up.
- Play in the IPTV player screen — opens in the shell overlay so **Back** returns to the catalog; the shell nav rail hides while the player is open (full-width video on TV/desktop). On **Android TV** playback uses the native ExoPlayer engine (reliable video surface); other platforms use MediaKit (libmpv). On **Windows**, live IPTV uses software decode (avoids a ~20s last-frame freeze with GPU decode). On **Android TV**, live channels use a larger live buffer and stay a few seconds behind the live edge; on **Android 7 and older** the player prefers up to **720p** / ~3.5 Mbps when the feed has adaptive quality levels (fixed single-bitrate channels still play as the portal sends them). Volume (including mute) is remembered on this device and reapplied after reconnects. If the feed drops, the player shows **Reconnecting…** and retries. With controls visible, D-pad **←/→** moves between chrome buttons; **→** from **Back** reaches the top-right actions (**Player** / source / stats / PiP when shown); **↓** from the top bar goes to the **progress bar** on VOD (or play on live); **↑** from any bottom transport button (including search, guide, fullscreen) returns to the progress bar on VOD, or **Back** on live; on the progress bar, **OK** arms scrub, **←/→** nudge, **OK** commits (**Back** cancels), **↑** returns to the top bar and **↓** to play. **OK** activates the focused control. When controls are hidden, **←/→** seek (VOD) and **↑/↓** adjust volume. On desktop, hover the volume icon to expand the volume bar (tap mutes; long-press pins the bar). The in-player **source** sheet lists are D-pad focusable; category **pin** is mouse-only on TV
- Use **Player**, **stream stats**, and **Picture-in-picture** from the IPTV player top-right (same flat controls as the movie player) on Android and desktop — **Player** opens built-in / external app options; **stream stats** (MediaKit) shows codec/bitrate/buffer; PiP hides chrome while active; on desktop, hover the PiP window and tap the PiP icon to restore. On **Android TV**, those top-right controls (and the multi-source chip) use the same brand-green D-pad focus as **Back** and the bottom transport row
- Change live channels from the in-player channel guide (groups + channel list overlay; roomier columns, taller rows, logos fitted without cropping) — on **Android TV**, **↑/↓** move the highlight, **←/→** switch between groups and channels (wide layout) or open the channel list (narrow); **Right** on the channel list closes the guide and returns focus to the player controls; **OK** / **Select** picks the focused group or tunes the focused channel; **Back** returns to groups (narrow) or closes the guide
- Search channels from the in-player search overlay (matches name or category) — dismiss with the borderless **Close** control in the header
- View programme guide (NOW / NEXT with progress) as a floating card at the bottom-right of the player when your portal provides EPG — tap **Read More** on the current programme description to expand it inline
- See the current channel logo at the bottom-left of the player; for VOD it sits immediately before the progress bar, with playback time on the bar's right
- Double-click the video (desktop) to enter/exit fullscreen — same as the fullscreen button

## Setup

1. Get portal URL, username, and password from your IPTV provider
2. Open **Portals** → **Add**, **Deal** when your account has Deal unlocked (spends 1 catalog credit for up to 5 pool portals), or **Scrape** / **Find Portals** when scrape is unlocked
3. To import from a share code: in the portal dialog (**Share code** title), paste a share token — either a new self-contained `F1.…` string, or a legacy **XXXX-XXXX** 8-character code into the tall squares — when the code resolves, the dialog asks for an optional **Portal name**, then connects after you confirm (or cancel to go back). Tap the **↓** control on the bottom edge to expand manual entry (title becomes **Add Portal**; optional **Portal name**, then URL, username, password as underline fields) — confirm with the green check icon or dismiss with the muted close icon at the bottom-right (only when expanded); use the header **X** to close without saving. **Edit portal** includes the same **Portal name** field. On the **web** remote settings page, add portals with the manual form, or **Export CSV** to download portals (credentials included) as a spreadsheet file; **Save** to sync.
4. Tap a portal in the list to load its catalog

## Tips

- Portal passwords on this device use Keychain / Keystore when allowed (macOS can choose a local app-file vault instead — no login-Keychain password prompts); CSV import/export files still contain passwords in plain text — keep those files private
- **Portals** panel on the right (desktop / Android TV) holds add and the full portal list (plus scrape when the account feature is on) — search filters by portal name, account name, or URL
- Section chips (**Live** / **Movies** / **Series**) switch the catalog; data stays on this device until you hit **Reload** on the selected shelf (or the centered **Reload** button when the catalog failed to load — autofocused on **Android TV**), or clear **IPTV portal cache** in Settings
- On **Android TV**, empty portals autofocus the first available CTA (**Deal** / **Find Portals** / **Add portal**)
- On **Android TV**, focusable controls (search, portals, dialogs, back/close, portal actions, group rows) use **brand green** when D-pad focused — **Live / Movies / Series** shelf chips keep their own section colors on focus (same as hover); the label goes **bold only while hovered or D-pad focused** (selection alone does not bold)
- On **Android TV**, search fields focus in browse mode first — press **Enter** on the remote to open the keyboard, then type
- On **Android TV**, D-pad from **Open portal** (no provider yet) opens the **Portals** panel with focus on **Add** (+)
- On **Android TV**, in the **Portals** panel: **Down** from the top-bar **Portals** button enters the header actions (**Add**); **Up** from header actions returns to the top-bar **Portals** button; **Down** from header actions enters the portal list; **Up** from the first portal row returns to **Add**; **Left** on a portal card is trapped (use **Back** to leave the panel); **Right** moves through **favorite → copy → edit → delete** (row actions stay open while those controls are focused); on delete confirm, **Right** moves **Yes → No**, and **No** returns focus to **Delete**; **Back** closes the panel and restores **category** focus
- On **Android TV**, **Add portal** dialog opens with focus on the manual-entry **↓** control (not the share-code keyboard); **Up** / **Down** move between share code and expand when collapsed — expand first to reach Portal name, URL, username, password, then the green **check** / muted **close** icons; after a share code resolves, focus lands on **Portal name** before connect; **Up** from share code does not move to the header **X** (use **Back** to dismiss); fields use an underline highlight on focus — press **Enter** to open the keyboard and type; **Edit portal** opens with Portal name highlighted (Enter to edit)
- On **Android TV**, top bar: **Portals** ← **Sort** (Live only) ← **Search** ← **Live / Movies / Series** (search field stays out of the chain until you activate search); **Down** from shelf tabs, **Search**, or **Sort** jumps to the **selected group** row in the catalog sidebar (when the **Portals** panel is closed, **Down** from **Portals** does the same; when the panel is open, **Down** from **Portals** enters the panel header actions instead)
- On **Android TV**, catalog: **Right** from a group row focuses the **channel** list (restores your last channel when possible); **Left** from the first column of channels (or any channel in the compact list) returns to that channel's **group** row in the sidebar; **Right** at the end of a channel row stays on that row (does not wrap to the next row); **Up** from the first channel row goes to the **active shelf tab** (left columns) or the **Portals** button (rightmost column); **Up** from the first group row returns to the active shelf tab
- On **Android TV**, choosing **IPTV** in the nav menu lands focus on the **first group row** once the catalog has loaded (spinner shown while loading)
- On **Android TV**, D-pad moves **Live / Movies / Series** → categories → streams; with the **Portals** panel open, **Right** from the stream grid (or portal button) enters the portal list; **Left** on a portal card stays in the panel (**Back** closes it and restores **category** focus)
- Press **Back** / **Escape** to close the Portals panel before leaving the tab
- While watching **live TV**, tap the grid icon in the player controls to open the channel guide
- Programme guide is optional — turn off **IPTV programme guide (EPG)** under Settings → Playback if you want zero EPG network requests
- **Deal** is available only when your signed-in account has `dealPortal` enabled (default off). It spends **1 credit** for up to **5** alive portals from the shared catalog pool (weighted lottery — less-dealt / recently checked hosts preferred, diverse hosts when possible; region `ANY`), and never more than your remaining portal slots. Credits are granted by admins; balance shows in Portals and on web **Account → IPTV** only when Deal is unlocked.
- Each profile may hold up to **5** Xtream portals by default (`features.maxIptvPortals`). Operators can raise that number per account in Features; **admin** accounts are unlimited. Add / import / scrape / Deal stop at the limit.
- **Scrape** / **Find Portals** is available only when your signed-in account has `iptvScrape` enabled in cloud account features (default off; guests never see it). When enabled, scrape walks Reddit IPTV communities only (GitHub XML2 dump scraping is disabled for now), and the web **Account → IPTV** page shows a VIP **Activated** banner for that perk. Scrape understands plain `get.php` links, Host/User/Pass cards (including common unicode spellings), and tabular `host:port user:pass` dumps — Stalker/MAC portal posts are ignored (Forja uses Xtream login, not device MAC). Operators fill the shared pool via [Catalog ops](iptv-catalog-ops.md).
- Portal quality varies — timeouts usually mean provider or network issues
- Clear stale alive checks / channel scan hits / **cached catalogs** from **Settings → Data & backup → IPTV portal cache** (portals and favorites stay; next IPTV open re-fetches catalogs)
- Export or import Xtream portals as CSV from **Settings → Data & backup → IPTV portals** when the IPTV tab is visible (same format as the web remote settings page; import adds only portals that are not already saved)
- Series VOD uses the same player as live with seek support when the stream allows — the VOD seek bar uses Forja brand green for the played fill and thumb
