# IPTV — Xtream

> Connect Xtream Codes, M3U playlists, and Stalker portals — live TV, VOD, series, and EPG (Xtream).

## What it is

The IPTV tab supports **Xtream Codes**, **M3U/M3U8** playlist portals, and **Stalker / Ministra**. The tab opens on the **catalog** (categories + channels). Pick a portal from the side panel, switch **Live / Movies / Series** from the top bar (Movies/Series for Xtream and Stalker), and play in the IPTV player.

## How to open it

Tap **IPTV** in the navigation bar. If you used a portal before, its catalog loads automatically; otherwise choose a portal from the **Portals** panel.

## What you can do

- Open the **Portals** panel from the top-bar portal control (status dot + portal name; hover/focus expands left to show connection seats `used/max`) — after a portal is active — to add, scrape, search, favorite, or switch providers; until then use **Open portal** in the empty state
- Switch **Live**, **Movies**, and **Series** from section chips in the top bar once a portal is active — the full top bar (shelf, search, portals) is hidden until you pick a provider
- Catalog is **cached on this device** per portal/section (disk + session) — quit and reopen reuses the last fetch for that shelf. Opening a portal (or switching **Live / Movies / Series**) loads **only that shelf**; other shelves load when you open them. **Reload** refreshes the active shelf from the portal. Login, catalogs, and series episodes are fetched and normalized in the Rust engine (Flutter only renders)
- Hover (or focus) the **selected** section chip (**Live** / **Movies** / **Series**) to reveal **Reload** and force a fresh catalog fetch for that shelf (updates the on-device cache). On **Android TV**, hold **OK** ~**1s** on a shelf chip to reveal **Reload**, then **→** to focus it; leaving the chip (or **→** from Reload to another shelf) hides it again
- Browse live channels, movies, and series by category — tiles and compact list rows show logos or posters fitted inside the card (live logos not cropped to fill), plus a play control on hover (desktop) or focus (TV); hover the play control to turn it brand-green, float it upward, and pulse the icon; a slightly brighter border appears on card hover/focus. Hover/focus also probes **Live** channels after a short pause (**350ms** dwell on **Android TV** — not every D-pad step) and tints the border green (alive) or red (dead); the result stays cached about **two minutes** (same window as portal status dots), then a later hover/focus re-checks and updates the border if the stream flipped. **Movies** and **Series** skip status checks. On **Android TV**, live logos sit in a centered square band above the title (Movies / Series keep poster cover); compact channel list rows are denser with square logos that stay undistorted; channel logos (and live NOW strips) load after D-pad focus is idle **500ms**; logos already on screen stay while you scroll — only new channels wait for the next idle settle; D-pad **←** from any category row returns to the nav rail, **OK** opens channels (**→** focuses the Live pin when shown, otherwise opens channels), and **↑** from the first category returns to Live / Movies / Series. Long category and channel lists jump focus into view instantly (no delayed highlight while scrolling); category rows use a fixed height so holding **↑** does not make the green left bar jump.
- On **desktop**, switch Live catalog between **Cards** and **EPG guide** with the pill next to Live / Movies / Series — guide shows a sticky channel column, half-hour time ruler, programme blocks sized by duration (6 hours behind · 24 hours ahead), and a red **Now** line; choice is remembered on this device; Movies / Series / compact keep cards only. On **TV** the EPG channel rail is D-pad focusable (**OK** plays); the favorite pin on a channel is mouse-only
- On **Live**, use the top-bar **Sort** control (between search and portals) to order **Categories** and **Channels** independently: playlist order, name A–Z, or name Z–A — remembered on this device; Movies/Series stay in portal order. On **Android TV**, **↑/↓** move one option at a time; **OK** applies the focused choice
- On **Live**, the category sidebar includes **Favorites** and **Already watched** above portal groups — star a channel to favorite it; opening a live channel adds it to Already watched (last 30 on this device, per portal). If the portal returns channels but no category list, the engine synthesizes a **Channels** (and **Uncategorized** when needed) group so Live is not stuck on empty Favorites
- On **Live**, press and hold a portal group row for about **1.5 seconds**, then drag to reorder the category list — the held row uses the same brighter-green rail highlight as Android TV (no floating card) while you move it (Favorites / Already watched stay fixed on top). Order is remembered per portal on this device when Sort → Categories is **Playlist Order**. Hover (or TV-focus) also shows a **pin** control that jumps a group to the top of the movable list; Movies / Series have no drag/pin. On **Android TV**, pin stays hidden while browsing (normal **→** opens channels); hold **OK** ~**1s** to lift the row (brighter green, same style — no card chrome) and reveal the pin (**→** focuses pin; pin **OK** pins/unpins, then focuses the row and scrolls the rail to it; **←** / **Back** returns), then **↑/↓** moves **one group at a time** while focus stays stuck on that floating row (channels cannot steal focus) until **OK** / **Back** / **←** drops it — the row stays where it is until dragging reaches the **second** visible slot, then the rail scrolls to keep it there until the list ends (first/last)
- **Search** live, movie, and series catalogs (search icon → slide-in bar on wide layouts, dialog on compact layouts; matches channel or group name globally, and the category sidebar lists only groups that contain hits — Favorites / Already watched appear when they have hits). Clearing search keeps the last category you picked or the category of a channel you played; otherwise it restores the category you had before search. After clearing search or returning from a watched channel, the sidebar scrolls to the selected category — on **desktop**, **Cmd+F** / **Ctrl+F** opens the search bar and focuses the field (same as the search icon; macOS **Edit → Find…** too)
- Watch VOD movies and series — open a movie or series for a **details** overlay like Home (same D-pad/scroll: TMDB backdrop when matched, overview, **Play**, Cast/Characters, Crew, Trailers, Recommendations; series also get season/episode cards). On **Android TV**, focus lands on **Play**; **↑** from Play focuses Back (scrolls hero into view when returning from episodes); **↓** reaches seasons/episodes then meta rows; remote **Back** pops details to the catalog
- View EPG (program guide) when the portal provides it — disable in **Settings → Playback → IPTV programme guide (EPG)** to skip loading guide data
- Favorite portals in the Portals panel — the star is hidden until you hover/focus a row (always visible when already favorited); favorited portals pin to the top with **gold** title text; below them, the most recently scraped, dealt, or added portals appear first (with a **NEW** badge on session-fresh rows until you hover or focus them — styling only, position stays put)
- Portal rows and the top-bar portal control show your **Portal name** when you set one (add / edit / share-code import); otherwise they fall back to the account name from the provider
- Portal rows show the subscription **end date** on the first line (green · orange · red by time left), a muted **platform badge** (**Xtream** · **M3U** · **Stalker**) before the server URL, and **connection seats** (`used/max`) under that (blue when free capacity, gray when full); the **status dot** on the top-bar portal control (and panel rows) probes when a portal becomes active — green/red stay cached about **two minutes**, then the active portal is re-checked automatically; each successful probe also refreshes **expiry** and **used/max** (saved on this device); **Reload** on a shelf also re-checks; idle panel rows probe on hover (desktop) or after **2 seconds** of D-pad focus (Android TV) when uncached; hover/focus reveals row actions (**copy share code / edit / delete**) and the favorite star (unless already starred) without moving the status icon off-center; confirming **delete** dims the row with diagonal stripes until it disappears (local inventory updates immediately; cloud sync continues in the background)
- **Copy share code** transforms the row into a share token (copied automatically); click the row again to restore portal details. Normal row click still selects the portal when the code is not shown. New shares are a self-contained `F1.…` token (no pastebin). Old 8-character codes still import when the legacy share host is up.
- Play in the IPTV player screen — opens in the shell overlay so **Back** returns to the catalog; the shell nav rail hides Offstage while the player is open (full-width video on TV/desktop; rail stays mounted so exit does not remount it). Opening the player also clears poster/image memory and unloads other shell tabs so live decode gets max resources (the IPTV catalog under the player stays mounted). On **Android**, **live** IPTV uses Settings → Playback → **IPTV engine** (or the in-player **Player** menu); **Movies/Series** use Settings → **Movies & series engine** (same as Home/Search — independent of live). Leaving the player silences and detaches the video surface before the route pops (avoids a freeze/force-close on Android TV after switching to MediaKit). Switching engines from that menu also unmounts the current surface, cools down briefly, then starts the other engine (so Android TV does not force-close mid-switch). On **Android TV**, that menu lists only ExoPlayer and MediaKit (no external apps); MediaKit uses MediaCodec embed so video is not black. Other platforms use MediaKit (libmpv). On **Windows**, live IPTV uses software decode (avoids a ~20s last-frame freeze with GPU decode). On **Android TV**, live Exo channels use TextureView so video and chrome stay visible; quality stays full unless you set **Settings → Playback → IPTV live max quality** (Auto = full quality; 1080p / 720p / 480p are opt-in only); MediaKit can match display refresh when **Settings → Playback → IPTV match display refresh** is on (off by default), and keeps the Android audio output attached after open so exit mute does not stick — and it never falls back to software decode on the TV (that path force-closed the app on UHD). **4K** live on Exo turns off live-edge speed catch-up so audio does not stutter (HD/FHD keep catch-up). Volume (including mute) is remembered on this device and reapplied after reconnects. On **desktop / phone**, hover the volume icon to expand the volume bar (tap mutes; long-press pins the bar). On **Android TV**, there is no volume button — use hardware / system volume. If the feed stalls or the buffer runs dry, the player shows **Buffering…** / **Reconnecting…** and on MediaKit **rejoins the live edge** when data returns (it does not silently replay the last ~15 seconds). The bottom **Replay** control on live MediaKit also rejoins the live edge (it does not tear down the player — that used to force-close Android TV after switching from ExoPlayer). When the stream recovers, reconnect banners dismiss (they do not stay over working video). With controls visible, D-pad **←/→** moves between chrome buttons; **→** from **Back** reaches the top-right actions (**Player** / stats / PiP when shown); **↓** from the top bar goes to the **progress bar** on VOD when shown (or play on live / on **Android TV** Exo where the progress row is hidden); **↑** from any bottom transport button (including search and guide) returns to the progress bar on VOD when shown, or **Back** on live / ATV Exo; on the progress bar (when shown), **←/→** seek ±10s (or nudge after **OK** arms scrub; **OK** commits, **Back** cancels), **↑** returns to the top bar and **↓** to play. **OK** activates the focused control. When controls are hidden, **←/→** seek (VOD), **↑** shows chrome and focuses **Back**, and **↓** shows chrome and focuses **Play** (hardware volume keys still adjust volume). When a stream has several sources, the **swap** control in the bottom row (the only source switcher — there is no title-bar chip) opens a floating **Source** menu like the other in-player menus: on **Android TV** it centers itself and lands focus on the source that is playing, **↑/↓** move the list, **OK** switches, and **Back** closes the menu without leaving the player. On **Android TV**, the fullscreen button is hidden (the player is already immersive); phone and desktop keep it
- Use **Player**, **stream stats**, and **Picture-in-picture** from the IPTV player top-right (same flat controls as the movie player) on Android and desktop — **Player** opens built-in engine options on Android (Exo / MediaKit; on **Android TV** those two only — no external apps) / external app options on phone and desktop; **stream stats** (MediaKit and ExoPlayer) shows codec/bitrate/buffer and on **Android TV** opens with D-pad focus on **Close** and keeps it while the panel is open (**OK** / Select or remote **Back** dismisses the panel and stays in the player — Back does not return to the catalog until the panel is already closed); PiP hides chrome while active; on desktop, hover the PiP window and tap the PiP icon to restore. On **Android TV**, those top-right controls use the same brand-green D-pad focus as **Back** and the bottom transport row — focus lands on **Play** after open; **→** from **Back** reaches **Player** (and **stream stats** on MediaKit); **←** returns the other way; **←/→** on **Play** / **Replay** / search / guide also use explicit edges (not only geometry); **↑** from **Play** / **Replay** focuses **Back**, and **↑** from Search / Guide / Source focuses **Player** (important on **Exo** live where the progress row is hidden); chrome stays up for **10 seconds** idle while you D-pad (hide deferred while a control still has focus); when chrome is hidden, **↑** focuses **Back**, **↓** focuses **Play**, and on VOD **←/→** seek ±10s (on live, **←/→** bring chrome back to **Play**)
- Change live channels from the in-player channel guide (flat translucent groups + denser channel list overlay; logos fitted in a square box without stretch — no frosted blur over the live video) — on **Android TV**, the guide is a **full-height left rail** flush to the left edge (top to bottom); on **desktop**, the floating guide runs from below the window chrome down to the bottom of the player; on phone it stays a shorter floating overlay. Hover and D-pad focus use **brand green** (left bar + label), matching the catalog sidebar. Hover or D-pad focus on a channel probes live status after a short pause (**350ms** dwell on **Android TV** — not every ↑/↓ step) and shows a green/red dot on the logo (same **two-minute** cache as catalog borders). On **desktop**, hovering a group only highlights it — it does not scroll the category list or switch channels; **click** to open its channels. On **Android TV**, **↑/↓** on groups only moves the highlight (channel pane shows “Press OK to open” until you commit); focus stays fully on-screen while scrolling (instant jump, no delayed highlight); **OK** or **Right** opens that group’s channels and moves into the list; channel logos load after D-pad focus is idle **500ms**; logos already on screen stay while you scroll — only new rows wait for the next idle settle; **←** returns to groups; **Right** on the channel list closes the guide and returns focus to the player controls; **OK** / **Select** on a channel tunes it; **Back** returns to groups (narrow) or closes the guide
- Search channels from the in-player search overlay (flat translucent panel; matches name or category) — dismiss with the borderless **Close** control in the header. On **Android TV**, **OK** on the search field (or keyboard Done) focuses the first result — it does not open the channel; **↑/↓** move through results; **OK** on a result tunes that channel; **Back** from the result list returns to the search field; **Back** on the field closes the search overlay (stays in the player)
- View programme guide (NOW / NEXT with progress) as a floating card at the bottom-right of the player when your portal provides EPG — tap **Read More** on the current programme description to expand it inline
- See the current channel logo at the bottom-left of the player, immediately before the progress bar, with time on the bar’s right — **movies/series** get a scrubber (MediaKit and ExoPlayer on Android TV) plus **Audio** / **Subs** (and **Episodes** on series); **seekable live** (stream reports a duration window) also uses the advancing scrubber but keeps Live chrome (no track buttons); pure live shows programme-guide progress when EPG is on (otherwise a full live-edge track). On **Android TV** with **ExoPlayer** live, that progress row is hidden (use ←/→ to seek VOD when controls are hidden)
- Hit **Reload** in the player to rejoin the live edge — it drops the buffered backlog so you jump back to now. If the picture is stalled and the flush does not bring it back within a few seconds, Forja reconnects the stream for you and shows the usual reconnect banner. Auto-reconnect policy is **Settings → Playback → IPTV live recovery** (Stable / Stable stall-test / Classic)
- Double-click the video (desktop) to enter/exit fullscreen — same as the fullscreen button. On **Android TV**, the fullscreen button is hidden (already immersive)

## Setup

1. Get portal URL, username, and password from your IPTV provider
2. Open **Portals** → **Add**, **Deal** when your account has Deal unlocked (spends 1 catalog credit for up to 5 pool portals), or **Scrape** / **Find Portals** when scrape is unlocked
3. To import from a share code: in the portal dialog (**Share code** title), paste a share token — either a new self-contained `F1.…` string, or a legacy **XXXX-XXXX** 8-character code into the tall squares — when the code resolves, the dialog asks for an optional **Portal name**, then connects after you confirm (or cancel to go back). Tap the **↓** control on the bottom edge to expand manual entry (title becomes **Add Portal**). Choose **Xtream**, **M3U**, or **Stalker**, optional **Portal name** (dice icon fills a random one), then the fields for that type (Xtream: URL / username / password; M3U: playlist URL + optional User-Agent; Stalker: portal URL + MAC [+ optional serial]) — confirm with the green check icon or dismiss with the muted close icon at the bottom-right (only when expanded); use the header **X** to close without saving. **Edit portal** includes the same **Portal name** field. On the **web** remote settings page, add portals with the manual form, or **Export CSV** to download portals (credentials included) as a spreadsheet file; **Save** to sync.
4. Tap a portal in the list to load its catalog

## Tips

- Signed-in **portals sync from the cloud** for your active profile (Xtream, M3U, and Stalker) — the device list is a cache. Cloud wins on sign-in / profile switch; only an explicit delete (or clear-all) removes portals from the account
- Portal passwords / Stalker MAC secrets on this device use Keychain / Keystore when allowed (macOS can choose a local app-file vault instead — no login-Keychain password prompts); CSV import/export files still contain secrets in plain text — keep those files private
- See also [IPTV — M3U](iptv-m3u.md) and [IPTV — Stalker](iptv-stalker.md) for type-specific setup
- **Scrape** / **Find Portals** is available only when your signed-in account has `iptvScrape` enabled in cloud account features (default off; guests never see it). When enabled, scrape walks Reddit IPTV communities only (GitHub XML2 dump scraping is disabled for now), and the web **Account → IPTV** page shows a VIP **Activated** banner for that perk. Scrape understands plain `get.php` links, Host/User/Pass cards (including common unicode spellings), and tabular `host:port user:pass` dumps — Stalker/MAC portal posts are still skipped by client scrape (add Stalker manually). Operators fill the shared pool via [Catalog ops](iptv-catalog-ops.md).
- **Portals** panel on the right (desktop / Android TV) holds add and the portal list (plus scrape when the account feature is on) — search filters by portal name, account name, or URL; the list stays lazy (only visible rows mount) so large scraped catalogs stay responsive
- Section chips (**Live** / **Movies** / **Series**) switch the catalog; data stays on this device until you hit **Reload** on the selected shelf (or the centered **Reload** button when the catalog failed to load — autofocused once on **Android TV**; **↑** to the portal chip, **←** to **Favorites** / groups when the sidebar is present, **Back** to the category rail), or clear **IPTV portal cache** in Settings
- On **Android TV**, empty portals autofocus the first available CTA (**Deal** / **Find Portals** / **Add portal**)
- On Live, if the portal catalog fails to load, **Favorites** and **Already watched** stay in the sidebar so D-pad can leave **Reload**
- On **Android TV**, focusable controls (search, portals, dialogs, back/close, portal actions, group rows) use **brand green** when D-pad focused — **Live / Movies / Series** shelf chips keep their own section colors on focus (same as hover); the label goes **extra-bold only while hovered or D-pad focused** (selection alone does not bold)
- On **Android TV**, search fields focus in browse mode first — press **Enter** on the remote to open the keyboard, then type
- On **Android TV**, D-pad from **Open portal** (no provider yet) opens the **Portals** panel with focus on **Add** (+); when portals already exist, opening the panel (OK on **Portals**) lands D-pad focus on the **selected** portal (first row when none is selected)
- On **Android TV**, in the **Portals** panel: **Down** from the top-bar **Portals** button enters the header actions (**Add**); **Up** from header actions returns to the top-bar **Portals** button; **Down** from header actions enters the portal list where you last were — on first entry, the **active** portal (or the **first** row when none is selected); **Right** from the stream grid / Portals button always enters on the active portal (after favorite / deal / scrape reorder, the list scrolls to keep the active row reachable so **↓** does not stick on the header buttons — never while you are scrolling the list); **Up** from the first portal row returns to **Add**; **Left** on a portal card is trapped (use **Back** to leave the panel); **↑/↓** scrolls the portal list without expanding row actions (instant jump, no page-append hitch); **Right** opens **favorite → copy → edit → delete** (row actions stay open while those controls are focused); **↑/↓** from a row action moves to the previous/next portal card (does not trap on the action); on delete confirm, **Right** moves **Yes → No**, and **No** returns focus to **Delete**; **Back** closes the panel and restores **category** focus
- On **Android TV**, **Add portal** dialog opens with focus on the manual-entry **↓** control (not the share-code keyboard); **Up** / **Down** move between share code and expand when collapsed — expand first to reach Portal name, URL, username, password, then the green **check** / muted **close** icons; after a share code resolves, focus lands on **Portal name** before connect; **Up** from share code does not move to the header **X** (use **Back** to dismiss); fields use an underline highlight on focus — press **Enter** to open the keyboard and type; **Edit portal** opens with Portal name highlighted (Enter to edit)
- On **Android TV**, top bar: **Portals** ← **Sort** (Live only) ← **Search** ← **Live / Movies / Series** (**↑** from Search / Sort / Portals / search **X** stays on that control; shelf tabs sit flush with the category column; activating **Search** opens the input and the keyboard, collapses the shelf to the **active** section only — e.g. **Live** — so the field is not hidden under the strip; when search is open, **→** from the field focuses the **X** close control; closing with **X**, **Back**, or Escape returns focus to the **Search** button and restores the full shelf); keyboard **Done** / submit focuses the **first matching channel**, or stays on the search field in browse mode when there are no hits (does not reopen the keyboard); **Down** from shelf tabs (and catalog open) focuses the **first pinned** group when any are pinned, otherwise the **first portal category** (skips Favorites / Already watched) — without scrolling that row to the top of the rail; **Down** from **Search**, **X**, or **Sort** still jumps to the **selected group** (when the **Portals** panel is closed, **Down** from **Portals** does the same; when the panel is open, **Down** from **Portals** enters the panel header actions instead)
- On **Android TV**, catalog: ↑/↓ on group rows only moves focus (channel pane shows “Press OK to open” until you commit — no logo thrash); **OK** opens that group and focuses its **channel** list — each group starts at its first channel (returning **Left** then **Right** in the same group restores your place); normal **→** opens that group’s channels (pin stays hidden); hold **OK** ~**1s** (Playlist Order, no search) lifts the group (brighter green) and reveals the **pin** — **→** focuses pin (**OK** pins/unpins, then focuses that group and scrolls the rail to its new spot; **←** / **Back** returns to the group); **↑/↓** moves one group at a time with focus stuck on the floating row, scrolling only once it reaches the **second** visible slot then keeping it there until the list ends; drop float with **OK** / **Back** / **←**; **Left** from the first column of channels (or any channel in the compact list) returns to that channel's **group** row in the sidebar; **Right** at the end of a channel row stays on that row (does not wrap to the next row); **Up** from the first channel row goes to the **active shelf tab** (left columns) or the **Portals** button (rightmost column); **Up** from the first group row returns to the active shelf tab. Long category, channel, and **Portals** lists show a thin green scrollbar so you can see how far down the list you are. Holding **↑/↓** speeds up through long Portals / category / channel lists (and the in-player channel guide) — floating category reorder still moves one group per key (no hold acceleration)
- On **Android TV**, remote **Back** in the player focuses the top-left **Back** control first (closes guide if open; search uses its own ladder — results → field → close); **Back** again returns to the catalog with focus on the channel you were watching (scrolled into view in its group — including after changing channel in the guide; Favorites / Already watched stay selected when that channel is still listed); **Back** from the channel grid focuses that group in the sidebar (scrolled into view); **Back** again moves to the nav rail
- On **Android TV**, choosing **IPTV** in the nav menu (or opening a portal) lands focus on the **first portal category** once the catalog has loaded — not **Favorites** or **Already watched** (spinner shown while loading)
- On **Android TV**, D-pad moves **Live / Movies / Series** → categories → streams; with the **Portals** panel open, **Right** from the stream grid (or portal button) enters the portal list on the active portal (first when none); **Left** on a portal card stays in the panel (**Back** closes it and restores **category** focus)
- Press **Back** / **Escape** to close the Portals panel before leaving the tab
- While watching **live TV**, tap the grid icon in the player controls to open the channel guide
- Programme guide is optional — turn off **IPTV programme guide (EPG)** under Settings → Playback if you want zero EPG network requests
- **Deal** is available only when your signed-in account has `dealPortal` enabled (default off). It spends **1 credit** for up to **5** alive portals from the shared catalog pool (weighted lottery — less-dealt / recently checked hosts preferred, diverse hosts when possible; region `ANY`), and never more than your remaining portal slots. Credits are granted by admins; balance shows in Portals and on web **Account → IPTV** only when Deal is unlocked.
- Each profile may hold up to **5** portals by default (`features.maxIptvPortals`). Operators can raise that number per account in Features; **admin** accounts are unlimited. Add / import / scrape / Deal stop at the limit and show a toast.
- Portal quality varies — timeouts usually mean provider or network issues
- Clear stale alive checks / channel scan hits / **cached catalogs** from **Settings → Data & backup → IPTV portal cache** (portals and favorites stay; next IPTV open re-fetches catalogs)
- Export or import portals as CSV from **Settings → Data & backup → IPTV portals** when the IPTV tab is visible (same format as the web remote settings page; includes `platform`; import adds only portals that are not already saved)
- Series VOD uses the same player as live with seek support when the stream allows — the VOD seek bar uses Forja brand green for the played fill and thumb
