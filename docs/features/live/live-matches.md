# Live Matches

> Watch live sports and events from aggregated stream sources.

## What it is

Live Matches pulls schedules and streams from sports APIs ([PPV](https://ppv.is) / `api.ppv.st`, [Streamed](https://streamed.pk/docs) / `streamed.pk`, [MutStreams](https://mut.st/) / mirrors on [mutgo.link](https://mutgo.link/), installed **Stremio** sport addons, and **Forja Sports** — today’s ESPN schedule matched to channels on your own Xtream portal). Browse by sport category, pick a match, and watch in an embedded WebView player (PPV / Streamed / Mut) or the native IPTV player (Stremio / Forja Sports HLS). Some third-party embeds cannot be replayed in the native mpv player because their HLS URLs are session-bound.

## How to open it

Tap **Live Matches** in the navigation bar.

## What you can do

- Use **Servers** to switch between All, PPV, Streamed, MutStreams, **Forja Live**, **Stremio**, or **Forja Sports**. Forja remembers the last Servers choice across app restarts (default **All**). **All** merges PPV · Streamed · **Forja Live** (enabled plugins only). MutStreams, Stremio, and Forja Sports stay their own servers. **Forja Live** loads enabled engine catalogs (TimStreams, StreamFree, **ESPN**, …); catalog chips filter the grid (e.g. **ESPN** shows today’s ESPN schedule on **Engine**). Picking one catalog chip loads only that source; **All** loads every enabled catalog. ESPN also enriches other catalogs with team names when **Catalog → ESPN** is on in Settings and **All** (or ESPN) is selected. Opening a card resolves streams through the paired live plugin. **Stream resolve** in **Settings → Forja Sports** chooses **Sniff** (embed WebView player, default) or **Engine** (Forja plugins → GOAT unlock → `/hls-proxy` into the IPTV player; on miss shows an error toast — never the site embed player). A **Sniff** / **Engine** pill next to **Servers** shows the active mode (tooltip links to Settings).
- Switch sport category circles (All merges the same sport across servers — e.g. PPV “Football” and Streamed “football” share one chip). When PPV and Streamed carry the same scheduled match, All also combines them into one card. **24/7** covers PPV’s 24/7 category (including `always_live` channels whose start/end times are stale) and Streamed always-on channels (no schedule date — e.g. Willow Cricket, Tennis Channel, Rally TV). Those appear only on the 24/7 chip — hidden from All and from other sports (card grid and timeline) — and stay tappable as LIVE
- On **phone / desktop**, switch between **card view** and **timeline view** with the button to the right of Refresh. Timeline is the default; Forja remembers this choice across app restarts. Timeline view places each **1-hour** group on a continuous clock — a 00:10 card sits at 00:00 on the ruler (not stuck at the top). Same-hour streams share one horizontal line (swipe sideways for more without moving time). Opening the timeline (or switching to it / reloading) lands on **now**, not the first card; click the playhead time badge to jump back to now anytime. Vertical scroll moves the clock; **Day / 12h / 6h** set how many hours one screen height covers. The ruler tints each day a different colour (today stays white), and a green **NOW** line marks the current time. Hovering a card highlights it in place and lifts just that card above overlapping neighbours (no scale). **Miscellaneous / Other** events that are still airing (e.g. a stage race that started hours ago) stay pinned on the NOW line instead of sitting in the past.
- On **TV (Android TV / leanback)**, Live Matches is **cards only** — no timeline view and no view toggle. **Servers** lists **All**, **Forja Live**, **Stremio**, and **Forja Sports** (PPV / Streamed / MutStreams stay desktop/phone). A saved PPV/Streamed/Mut preference is clamped to **All**. The **Sniff** / **Engine** pill still shows next to **Servers**. Forja Live cards show a green source badge (TimStreams, StreamFree, …). Opening a match from **All** never shows PPV / Streamed rows — the picker only offers **Forja Sports** (Xtream channel matches) and **Stremio** (matching live-addon streams) in the same right-side panel. Forja Sports finishes matching first, then Stremio fills in (avoids overloading leanback sets). Match and channel tiles are **landscape** (same continue-watching width, 16:9 art + title/league under — not square/portrait IPTV poster cells). Sport chips sit flush left with **Servers** and the card grid (scaled to fit when needed). D-pad moves **Servers → Portals (Forja Sports) → Refresh → sport chips → match cards**. Focused **Servers** / **Portals** use the brand-green hover chrome; focused **Refresh** turns white. Within the card grid, **←/→** stay on the current row (row ends do not wrap). **↑** from the first card row returns to chips (not skipping to Servers). **←** from the left edge of a row returns to the nav rail. Empty lists autofocus **Refresh**. Entering the tab restores the last grid focus
- Browse upcoming and live events — live matches appear first (PPV **Live now** treats any active viewer count as live and playable — including early lobbies and wrong device clocks — until a few hours past the scheduled end; Streamed also merges the site’s **live** feed with the full schedule, so Popular Live rows like ACA / day-long golf are not missing). Only **live** matches are tappable and show the play button (green and floated upward, with a slowly pulsing play icon, on hover/focus). Upcoming cards still highlight and lift on hover in the timeline; they show the start time badge only. PPV cards show a live viewer count when the API reports one. Card and timeline views refresh ● LIVE badges about once a minute while the tab is open.
- Open a match and watch the stream. On **desktop / iOS**, PPV, Streamed, and MutStreams open in the embed WebView (while loading, a title strip sits **below** the window traffic lights / status bar; after load the video is full-bleed with floating **Back**, **Mute**, and PPV / Streamed / Mut badge over the picture at the same inset as the film player; autoplay when the embed allows it; Forja then forces unmute after load — including dismissing site overlays like MutStreams’ **CLICK UNMUTE STREAM**). On **TV**, after load the same floating **Back** sits over the video and bottom **Play** / **Mute** stay below so the remote is not stolen: focus lands on **Play**; **↑** reaches **Back**; **←/→** moves **Mute**; audio starts unmuted the same way. Select on Play/Pause drives the embed via a media bridge. On **Android TV**, remote **Back** twice leaves the embed (**OK** on the Back icon also leaves); remote **Exit** is separate (double-confirm quit). On **Android / Android TV**, System WebView cannot play those embeds in-page — Forja shows **Opening stream…** while it captures the HLS playlist (and cookies), then switches to the **native IPTV player**. That native player has the usual IPTV chrome, including the top-right **Player** control (ExoPlayer / MediaKit on Android TV; built-in engine / external apps on phone) — on **Android TV**, focus lands on **Play**; **←/→** moves **Replay** and top-bar **Player** (the embed WebView kept under Streamed/Mut for CDN fetches does not steal the remote). Some non-embedindia PPV streams open the native IPTV player directly after resolve. **Stremio** sport streams that return direct HTTP(S) HLS open the native IPTV player immediately (premium / bait links are skipped). On **Android / Android TV**, those Stremio streams keep addon request headers (and a streamed.pk Referer for Highfly leaf CDNs) so ExoPlayer can demux HLS URLs that omit a `.m3u8` suffix. **Forja Sports** ranks matched channels by confidence (4K + both teams, both in name and EPG, both combined, then nickname-only), opens a **right-side channel panel** immediately while searching (phase labels like **Searching Forja Sports…**, skeleton placeholders, then channels appear as they land — logos, short title, category, **T1–T4** badge), then opens the chosen channel in the native IPTV player (other matches stay available under **Source** with the same row layout). Hover or focus a channel row for a moment to **alive-check** it — green border/dot means the stream responds, red means dead (same dwell probe as IPTV Live). Matched channels for a game stay cached **30 minutes** so re-opening the same card does not re-search the portal.
- Back / Escape exits the embed and **stops audio** — while loading, Back lives in a strip above the WebView; after load it floats over the video with an opaque hit target so desktop taps still reach Flutter (WKWebView/WebView2 otherwise steal clicks). On **Android TV**, remote **Back** twice leaves (**OK** on the Back icon also leaves); remote **Exit** is separate
- On **Android TV**, pressing **Home** (or switching to another app) **pauses** the live stream; returning to Forja resumes. Desktop can keep playing in the background.
- When a match has several streams, opening the card **Resolving streams…** loads every catalog mirror in parallel, drops dead listings (Streamed mirrors with no `/api/stream/…` rows), and shows one merged picker — **Admin · English**, **Delta · English**, TimStreams rows, and so on — sorted by viewer count (busiest first). Tap a row to play (**Engine** opens the IPTV player via GOAT/`/hls-proxy`, or toasts on miss; **Sniff** uses the embed player). Streamed rows show HD and viewers when the API reports them. On **TV**, the first row autofocuses; **↑/↓** moves the list. Focused rows use a flat highlight (background fill, no zoom) with bold white title text — same for the **Servers** picker. **Stremio** matches with several streams instead open in the native player, where the **swap** control in the bottom row opens the **Source** menu (on TV it centers, focuses the stream that is playing, and takes **↑/↓** / **OK**)
- Double-click the video to enter/exit fullscreen (desktop **window** fullscreen for embeds — on macOS the site’s own HTML5 fullscreen is blocked so WebKit cannot crash the app; native PPV streams use the IPTV player). On **TV**, there is no fullscreen button — the player is already immersive
- Refresh lists for new events

## Tips

- Streams are third-party — availability changes with broadcasts and region
- Streamed / PPV embeds play inside a WebView on **desktop / iOS**. Forja wraps the embed in an iframe under the catalog site (`streamed.pk` / `ppv.is`) so `document.referrer` matches the website; ad scripts that block the player page are filtered. The catalog root is always allowed so the wrapper can paint; other main-frame navigations (ad click hosts, and the embed host itself on desktop/iOS) are cancelled so the player cannot jump out of the catalog wrapper into a host-lock / error page. On **Android / Android TV**, System WebView cannot play those embeds in-page (CORS + host lock). Forja shows **Opening stream…** while it captures the HLS playlist (and session cookies), then switches to the **native IPTV player** (remembers Live’s own ExoPlayer / MediaKit choice — ExoPlayer until you change it) via a local proxy that sends Referer + cookies for **PPV**, or (for **Streamed**) by playing a local playlist while the embed WebView fetches the real CDN segments — use **Player** there to switch ExoPlayer / MediaKit (on Android TV those two only; phone also lists external apps). Streamed still uses the catalog iframe wrapper for sniff; PPV `embedindia` loads top-level with a `ppv.is` Referer header so the sniff is not blocked by cross-origin iframe rules. Under that cover, Forja starts JW muted and taps the player center (many PPV embeds only request HLS after play); phone taps also pass through the cover to the WebView. If capture fails or the playlist is not valid, you get a short toast and return to the match list
- Ad popups are accepted off-screen (required by some Streamed embeds) and never shown over the player; main-frame ad redirects are still cancelled
- WebView playback may behave differently per platform; some embeds still require a tap if the site blocks unmuted autoplay
- On Windows, Live Matches uses the same iframe wrapper + hidden ad `window.open` host as other desktop platforms, and forces an opaque WebView2 surface so the window does not go white/transparent
- Escape still backs out of the embed player on all platforms (and stops the stream)
