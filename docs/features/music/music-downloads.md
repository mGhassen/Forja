# Music downloads

> Save tracks for offline listening.

## What it is

Download individual tracks from the Music section to your device storage. Downloaded tracks appear in your offline library and play without streaming.

## How to open it

From **Music** — download action on tracks (where offered in the UI).

## What you can do

- Download tracks for offline playback
- Play downloaded files from your saved library
- Manage storage used by downloads (device settings)

## Tips

- Respect copyright and terms of your music sources
- Downloads use device storage — clear old tracks periodically on phones
