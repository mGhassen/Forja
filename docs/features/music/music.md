# Music

> Search, stream, and collect music with lyrics and a full player.

## What it is

The Music tab uses Deezer metadata for search, trending, playlists, and album art. Play tracks in a full-screen player with art, synced lyrics, related tracks, shuffle, repeat, and queue. Like tracks and save albums to your library inside Forja.

## How to open it

The **Music** tab is temporarily hidden from the navigation bar and Settings → Features.

## What you can do

- Search artists, albums, and tracks
- Browse trending and editorial playlists
- Play with full transport controls (play/pause, skip, seek) — progress bar and thumb use Forja brand green
- View synced lyrics when available
- Like tracks and save albums
- Open the dedicated music player with queue
- Shuffle and repeat modes
- Background audio on mobile (continues when app is backgrounded)

## Tips

- Playback streams from source URLs resolved per track — requires network
- Use [Music downloads](music-downloads.md) for offline listening
