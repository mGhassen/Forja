# Simkl

> Sync your watch history with Simkl.

## What it is

Simkl is an alternative tracking service. Log in via OAuth device flow and sync what you watch between Forja and your Simkl account.

## How to open it

**Settings → Accounts & Sync → Simkl**

## What you can do

- Connect with Simkl device code
- View logged-in username
- Sync watch activity
- Log out

## Setup

1. Tap connect in Settings
2. Visit the verification URL with the displayed code
3. Wait for app to confirm login

## Tips

- You can use Simkl alongside or instead of [Trakt](trakt.md) depending on preference
- Not all Trakt features (calendars, lists UI) are duplicated for Simkl
