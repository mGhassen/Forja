# Audio tracks

> Pick your language and avoid codecs your device can't decode.

## What it is

Many streams include multiple audio tracks (languages, commentary, Atmos). Forja lets you set a preferred audio language in Settings and optionally avoid unsupported formats like Atmos, TrueHD, or 7.1 when your device or player can't handle them. You can also switch tracks manually in the player.

## How to open it

- **Settings → Playback** — preferred language and avoid unsupported audio
- **Player bottom bar** — audio button (next to subtitles) opens the audio track popup

## What you can do

- Set preferred audio language (or None for default)
- Toggle "avoid unsupported audio" to skip problematic codecs
- Manually select any available track in the player

## Tips

- "Avoid unsupported audio" helps on TVs and phones that choke on TrueHD/Atmos
- Track auto-select runs when a new stream starts
