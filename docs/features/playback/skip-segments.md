# Skip segments

> Skip intros, recaps, and credits when segment data is available.

## What it is

Forja integrates IntroDB to detect common TV segments — opening intros, "previously on" recaps, and end credits. When data exists for your show, skip buttons appear at the right moments.

## How to open it

Automatic during playback for supported series — look for skip prompts on the player overlay.

## What you can do

- Skip intro segment
- Skip recap segment
- Skip credits (jump toward next episode or end)

## Tips

- Coverage varies by show — not every series is in IntroDB
- You can always seek manually if no skip button appears
