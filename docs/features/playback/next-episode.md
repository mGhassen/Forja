# Next episode

> Binge TV without leaving the player.

## What it is

When you finish an episode (or near the end), Forja offers to play the next episode — for TMDB TV in the main player and for dedicated hub players (anime, Arabic, Asian drama). Reduces friction for serial watching.

## How to open it

Automatic at end of episode or via the next-episode prompt on the player.

## What you can do

- Tap to play the next episode in sequence
- Dismiss and stay on credits or exit

## Tips

- Hub players (Anime, KissKH, etc.) have their own episode chains separate from TMDB TV
- Watched state updates when you complete an episode
