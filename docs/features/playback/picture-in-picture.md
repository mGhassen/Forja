# Picture-in-picture

> Keep watching in a small window while you use other apps (Android).

## What it is

On Android, Forja supports system picture-in-picture. Minimize the player to a floating window and continue playback while browsing or chatting. Returning to the app restores full-screen playback.

## How to open it

Use the PiP button in the player (Android) or enter PiP when leaving the app during playback (device-dependent).

## What you can do

- Shrink video to a corner overlay
- Resume full screen when tapping the PiP window
- Continue audio/video in the background briefly

## Tips

- PiP availability depends on Android version and OEM behavior
- Not available on desktop; use window resizing instead
