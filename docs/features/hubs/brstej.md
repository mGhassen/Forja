# Brstej

> Browse Arabic series from Brstej.

## What it is

The **Brstej** tab is a catalog hub from the ForjaHQ **Brstej** pack (`brstej-hub`). It browses [Brstej](https://uo.brstej.com/) series — spotlight hero, Continue Watching, latest, and a ranked series rail. Playback uses the **Brstej** Forja provider plugin (direct HLS/MP4 after server unlock). The host renders Catalog Shell with `surface: arabic`. The tab appears in **Settings → Features** while the Brstej pack is enabled under **Forja Packs → Hubs** (defaults off).

## How to open it

Enable the ForjaHQ Brstej pack under **Settings → Forja Packs → Hubs**, turn **Brstej** on in **Settings → Features**, then tap **Brstej** in the navigation bar.

## What you can do

- Browse latest Brstej series
- Search Brstej titles (episodes grouped into shows)
- Open details, pick an episode, and play a direct stream

## Tips

- Use **Settings → Forja Packs → Refresh** after a pack update so browse/extract scripts reload
- Larozaa movies/series stay on the [Arabic](arabic.md) tab
- Arabic cartoons stay on **[كرتون](cartoon.md)**
