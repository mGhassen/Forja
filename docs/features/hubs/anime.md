# Anime

> Dedicated anime hub — discover, search, and multi-server playback.

## What it is

The Anime tab is a full vertical for anime: hero carousel, mood chips, continue watching, curated rails (trending, airing, etc.), discover filters, search, and a dedicated player that races every available stream source in parallel (Kaiju/Ronin via Anikoto, plus Miruro servers Chibi/Nori/Tofu/…).

## How to open it

Tap **Anime** in the navigation bar.

## What you can do

- Browse hero and mood-based rails
- Continue watching in-progress series
- Discover with filters
- Search anime catalog
- Open details with episodes
- Toggle **SUB** / **DUB** before playback
- Play in the anime player — all servers are tried in parallel; switch sources in the player (e.g. **Kaiju · SUB**, **Miso · SUB**, **Shiro · SUB**)
- Track watch history per series (sub/dub preference)

## Tips

- Anime uses its own player and history — separate from TMDB TV details
- **Kaiju** / **Ronin** need Anikoto catalog linkage; Miruro servers (Chibi, Nori, …) work from AniList id alone
- Playback shows a backdrop + title loading screen before the player opens (same pattern as films and Asian drama)
- Part of [content hub scrapers](../scrapers/content-hub-scrapers.md)
