# Anime

> Dedicated anime hub — discover, search, and multi-source playback.

## What it is

The Anime tab is a full vertical for anime: hero carousel, mood chips, continue watching, curated rails (trending, airing, etc.), discover filters, search, and a dedicated player that races multiple sources for the fastest stream.

## How to open it

Tap **Anime** in the navigation bar.

## What you can do

- Browse hero and mood-based rails
- Continue watching in-progress series
- Discover with filters
- Search anime catalog
- Open details with episodes
- Play in the anime player with source failover
- Track watch history per series

## Tips

- Anime uses its own player and history — separate from TMDB TV details
- Part of [content hub scrapers](../scrapers/content-hub-scrapers.md)
