# Anime Arabic

> Arabic-dubbed anime via AnimeSlayer.

## What it is

Anime Arabic is a hub for Arabic-dubbed anime powered by AnimeSlayer. Hero section, continue watching, search, details, and a dedicated player for dubbed episodes.

## How to open it

Tap **Anime Arabic** in the navigation bar.

## What you can do

- Browse featured and continue watching
- Search Arabic anime catalog
- Open series details and episode list — tap an episode card to select it; click the card’s **play** button (or hero **Play** / **Resume**) to start that episode
- Play episodes in the Arabic anime player
- Resume from per-series history
- Hover a continue watching card (desktop) to scale it and show a brand-green play button that floats upward while its play icon pulses slowly

## Tips

- Content is Arabic dub focused — subtitles depend on source
- Independent from the main [Anime](anime.md) tab
