# Arabic cinema

> Browse and watch Arabic movies and series by category.

## What it is

The Arabic tab offers category browsing, search, liked titles, and a dedicated player for Arabic cinema content. Built for regional films and series outside the main TMDB torrent flow.

## How to open it

The **Arabic** tab is temporarily hidden from the navigation bar and Settings → Navigation.

## What you can do

- Browse by category
- Search titles
- Like movies for quick access
- Open details and play in the Arabic player
- Track viewing per title

## Tips

- Uses hub-specific scrapers — not the same as TMDB [Media details](../movies-tv/media-details.md)
- Pair with [Anime Arabic](anime-arabic.md) for dubbed anime
