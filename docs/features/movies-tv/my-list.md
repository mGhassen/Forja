# My List

> Bookmark movies and shows to watch later.

## What it is

My List is your personal bookmark grid inside Forja. Save TMDB titles and Stremio items you want to come back to. Remove items with undo support if you tap by mistake.

## How to open it

Tap **My List** in the navigation bar.

## What you can do

- View all bookmarked movies and series in a grid
- Tap a poster to open details and play
- Remove items from the list (with undo)
- Add items from any details screen via the list/bookmark action

## Tips

- My List is stored locally on your device
- Use [Backup & restore](../settings/backup-restore.md) to move lists to another install
