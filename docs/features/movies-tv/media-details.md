# Media details

> Full movie and series page — metadata, torrents, streams, episodes, and lists.

## What it is

When you open a title from Home, Discover, Search, or lists, you get the **media details** screen — one screen for torrents, Stremio/Nuvio/Forja, and webstreaming. A **cinematic hero** (~82% of the viewport) shows a backdrop that **rotates random TMDB stills** for the title when several exist — with a slow **Ken Burns** pan/zoom on desktop/mobile (static stills on **Android TV** so the set does not stutter) — then a chromeless YouTube trailer after a random **12–20 seconds** (preloaded for a smooth crossfade). After each trailer finishes, the backdrop returns for another random 12–20 seconds, then the trailer plays again. Trailer audio is on by default and fades from quiet to full volume over 3 seconds. A **mute** button in the bottom-right of the hero toggles sound.

On **TV series**, the backdrop continues below the hero chrome so season and episode rails sit on the image — not on a separate black block. The episode rail reserves ~500px of backdrop when seasons are shown, or ~320px for episodes-only (single-season titles) so the row sits closer under Play. Hero chrome is a bit taller than a single viewport so seasons/episodes sit slightly lower. Synopsis stays as **3 lines + Read More** (same as movies) when there is room; on short phones **and short landscape panels (e.g. 720p Android TV)** the chrome floor is taller and secondary chrome (director, providers, genres, then synopsis) yields first so **title** and **Play** / **Resume** stay visible.

The hero overlay is split into two columns on wide screens:

- **Top-left back chevron** — muted until hover or D-pad focus (then white); tap / click / TV **OK** pops the shell overlay back to the previous screen. On **Android TV**, remote **Back** focuses the chevron first when another control is focused; **Back** again pops to the catalog and restores D-pad on the last selected card (scroll stays). On **desktop**, the mouse **Back** side button, **Escape**, and a two-finger trackpad swipe-right on page chrome (left-edge arrow fills to commit; horizontal strips only scroll) do the same. From the leftmost hero action (**Play** / **Resume**), **↑** focuses the back chevron and **←** moves focus to the shell nav rail (same as Home hero); **→** on the active nav tab returns focus to the details page. On **Android TV**, leaving the player or canceling stream loading returns D-pad focus to **Play** / **Resume**.
- **Left:** TMDB logo or stylized title (on **TV**, plain white text when there is no logo — no cyan/amber chromatic offset, which looked doubled on Android TV), inline genres (`Horror • Thriller`), year · runtime · certification · rating, director line, synopsis (left **40%** of the screen), then green **Play** / **Resume** with play icon when **Webstreaming** is on, or when Forja **Auto** is checked with Webstreaming off (races Forja plugins), white **Play** / **Resume** with link icon (opens the **Sources** panel for torrent / Stremio / Nuvio / Forja), a trash icon next to Resume when you have watch progress (clears that title/episode from history **and** the cached webstreaming provider extract so Play re-resolves), **Trailer** (when TMDB has videos — opens the in-app trailer player on the best match), and a glass **+** for My List (floating status menu: Plan to Watch / Watching / On Hold / Completed / Dropped — full-row hover with green); watch progress when you have history appears below the actions.
- **Right:** production facts panel (no heading) — status, language, and TV fields (first/last aired, seasons, episodes, network, production companies, origin, creators) or movie fields (release date, runtime, production, origin, budget/revenue) from TMDB rich details.

Hero text sits in the **upper** area of the hero (not pinned to the bottom). Cast, trailers, and recommendations scroll on the flat shell background (`#141414`), with equal vertical spacing between the episode rail and each body section.

Scroll below the hero for:

1. **Episodes** (TV only) — on the **extended page backdrop** under the hero chrome: horizontal season poster cards (when the show has more than one season), then a horizontal episode rail (skeleton cards while a season is loading); season cards show a highlighted border on hover (desktop) or D-pad focus (TV), and the selected season keeps the same border; episode thumbs likewise show a border when focused or selected (no scale-up on TV); **selecting a season updates the hero backdrop** to that season's poster (falls back to the show backdrop when no season art exists); when a season has more than 50 episodes, a range dropdown (**1 - 50**, **51 - 100**, …) sits on the **Episodes** title row (opposite the title). Each episode card shows its air date when TMDB provides one; upcoming (not yet aired) dates appear in **orange** and cannot be opened. Tap a card to select an episode; click its play button to play or resume. On **Android TV**, **OK** on an episode selects it and moves focus to the play icon; **OK** on the play icon starts or resumes webstreaming. Opening a show without a Continue Watching deep link selects season 1 unless you have an in-progress episode (2–90% watched), in which case that season and episode are selected.
2. **Cast** — circular photos, actor and character names
3. **Trailers** — horizontal row of YouTube trailers/teasers from TMDB; tap to open the in-app trailer player (fullscreen over the shell — nav rail hidden; seek bar, ±10s skip, horizontal volume bar, subtitles, quality, playback speed, fullscreen on desktop). Playback resolves a direct YouTube stream and plays it in the native player (`contain` fit — letterboxing when needed, no zoomed crop). Opens a muxed progressive stream first (fast audible start, often ~360p); the Quality menu lists adaptive heights up to 1080p+ when YouTube offers them. If an adaptive Quality pick fails to attach audio/video, the player stays on (or returns to) muxed. If YouTube age-gates the stream (`Sign in to confirm your age`), the player falls back to the in-app YouTube embed so the trailer still plays (Forja is +18 — no YouTube account needed). Stream resolve starts in the background when the trailers row (or hero Trailer) appears so open is usually a cache hit; subtitles load when you open the CC menu. On **desktop** and **TV**, trailer thumbnails use the same white focus/hover ring as poster cards (ring on the image only — not around the title below). Top-right **Player** (same control as the movie player) can hand the trailer off to the **YouTube** app. Desktop chrome auto-hides after idle like the main player (move/click to bring it back); double-click the video toggles fullscreen. When a title has more than one trailer, a bottom-right **More videos** card sits above the progress bar: hover lifts it (episode-card style), ←/→ chevrons alone slide between trailers, and a center play control (white; brand green on hover, same as catalog cards) plays the selected trailer — clicking the card body does not advance. When a trailer ends and another remains, the card becomes **Up next** with a 5s countdown and then auto-plays (click plays immediately; seek/replay cancels). On **Android TV**, D-pad moves focus between chrome controls (back, More videos, progress bar, play, skip, subtitles, quality, speed); on More videos **←/→** change the preview and **OK** plays it, **↑** focuses Back, **↓** focuses the progress bar; on the progress bar **OK** arms the thumb, **←/→** nudge, **OK** commits; **↑** from the bar focuses More videos when shown, otherwise Back; **↑** from any transport control returns to the progress bar. Subtitles, quality, and speed menus use the same option chips as the main player (language flags on subs; tap applies immediately). **Back** (remote) focuses the top-left Back control first (or dismisses an open menu); a second **Back** exits the player to the details page. Remote **Exit** is separate — double-confirm quit from the trailer (same as the rest of the app).
4. **More Like This** — recommendation row

Torrent search, Stremio, Nuvio, and Forja streams share one **Sources** list in a frosted right-side panel (BackdropFilter glass over the details page). Stream scrapes load **only when you open Sources** (white Play / Resume with link icon) on that kind — not when you only select an episode. When a play source is on in Settings, opening the title **prepares** its Sources chrome (kind tabs + provider chip lists for Stremio addons / Nuvio scrapers / Forja plugins) so the panel does not pop tabs or chips in after you open it. Opening details also does **not** wait on Stremio addon manifest fetches for the page itself (dead or slow addons hydrate in the background; the page shows TMDB as soon as meta is ready). Each kind appears only when its **Play source** is on in Settings (**Direct torrent**, **Stremio**, **Nuvio**, **Forja**). On **Android TV**, those toggles are in **Settings → Playback** like other platforms. Direct HTTP Stremio/Nuvio/Forja rows play on the TV; picking a torrent or magnet asks a paired desktop under **Settings → LAN** to run P2P (if you are not paired, or the desktop is offline, a dialog explains that). The panel opens on **Torrents** when Direct torrent is enabled, otherwise **Nuvio**, then **Forja**, then **Stremio** — there is no **All** kind. Switching kind loads that category if it is not already cached; leaving a kind while it is still loading **stops** that fetch so it cannot finish in the background. **Torrents** search the selected indexer (default: first enabled); tap **All** to search the rest. **Stremio** fetches only the selected addon. Results stay cached for about **30 minutes** for the same title/episode, so closing and reopening Sources (including in the player after you already loaded Forja/Nuvio on details) does not re-search. Closing the panel (click outside, **Escape**, or **Back** — there is no title or close **X** in the panel) **stops** any still-running Torrents / Stremio / Nuvio / Forja checks so they do not keep loading in the background. While that category is still fetching, the selected tab shows an animated **…** after the name; hover it to show **X** and cancel that fetch. Each provider chip that is still checking (indexer, Stremio addon, Nuvio scraper, or Forja plugin) shows the same **…**. The **selected** category tab shows a **reload** icon (spins on hover) to force a fresh fetch for that open category only — on **Android TV**, hold **OK** on that tab for 1 second (the icon is not D-pad focusable). New torrent/stream rows append without jumping the list back to the top — scroll and hover stay where you left them (switching Torrents / Stremio / Nuvio / Forja still starts at the top). On **TV**, **Torrents / Stremio / Nuvio / Forja** tabs are D-pad focusable. Opening or reloading **Stremio** re-reads installed stream addons for the plugin chip row. Search and Filters stay in the chrome even when the list is still empty. Leaving the details page also cancels in-flight checks. The panel chrome is compact: **Forja / Torrents / Stremio / Nuvio** tabs when enabled (episode label and result count on their own line above the tabs), then a horizontal **provider chip** row (**All** + enabled torrent providers / Jackett / Prowlarr, Stremio addons, Nuvio scrapers, or Forja plugins), then search + **Filters**. On **Forja**, chips soft-filter by media type (Movie on movies, TV on series); open **Filters → Category** to also show **Anime** / **Drama** (or the other of Movie/TV) without leaving the title — selected off-category chips stay usable. Torrents **All** highlights every enabled built-in provider chip (Jackett / Prowlarr stay separate). Opening Torrents searches the first enabled indexer; tap **All** to search every enabled chip. Tap **All** again to clear every built-in chip. On **desktop**, source rows, provider chips, filter chips, and the tune / audio / copy-magnet controls use the same ink hover as other shell lists. Torrents and Stremio show every row returned by their fetch. Nuvio with **All** selected loads scrapers in groups of 5 until every selected one has been tried. **Forja** with **All** selected walks selected plugins one at a time on the warm singleton runtime (HTTP links only — magnets are dropped); rows appear as each plugin finishes, and only the currently checking plugin chip shows **…**. Tap a scraper/plugin chip to load just that one (switching chips **stops** a still-running load; tap it again to continue). Reopening Sources continues any selected scrapers/plugins that have not been fetched yet. **Nuvio** appears when the Nuvio play source is on. **Forja** appears when the Forja play source is on (engine HTTP plugins; not green Play) — pack metadata for chips is prepared when the details page opens if Forja is enabled; plugin scrapes still run only when you open that tab or tap a chip. **Filters** opens as a second full-height frosted panel docked to the left of Sources (same top/bottom edge) only when you tap the tune control — not automatically with Sources, and not as a bottom sheet. Filters only covers the area left of Sources, so Torrents / Stremio / Nuvio / Forja rows stay clickable. You can close Filters with the tune control or its close button; closing Sources also dismisses Filters. Sources stays usable while Filters is open. In the player, Sources and Filters use a translucent dark shell (no freeze-frame image; no BackdropFilter over live video). Each torrent/Stremio/Nuvio/Forja row uses the same card: **title** on the left with **provider** and seed count (↑ number, no badge) stacked on the right, then a meta row with language **flags** as plain emoji (no pill) plus badges for **quality**, **file size** (scraper / Torrentio `behaviorHints.videoSize` / size token in stream title), codec, and tech tags when known. **Webstreaming** is started from the hero green **Play** / **Resume** (play icon) — it is not in Sources. With Webstreaming off, the same green button can run Forja when **Auto** is checked on the Forja play-source row (Settings → Playback). Only play sources enabled in **Settings → Playback** appear in the panel (Direct torrent → torrent providers; Stremio → addon streams; Nuvio → scrapers; Forja → Forja plugins).

## How to open it

Tap any movie or series poster from Home, Discover, Search, or lists.

## What you can do

- Watch the hero backdrop (Ken Burns pan/zoom on desktop/mobile; stills on Android TV) for 12–20s, then chromeless autoplay trailer when TMDB has one; alternates after each trailer ends (sound on, volume ramps up; mute toggle bottom-right)
- Green **Play** / **Resume** (play icon) auto-extracts the best direct webstreaming link when **Webstreaming** is on (admin) — that path URL-sniffs. When Webstreaming is off and **Auto** is checked on the Forja play-source row, the same green button uses the Sources → Forja loader (session cache, first working stream) — shared with Anime and Asian Drama. If that stream fails to open, Forja Auto tries up to **2 more** from the same plugin (3 opens max), then tells you to open **Sources** and pick manually. Fresh installs default to **Forja** only (Webstreaming off).
- If Play can’t start a stream, the cinematic loading screen stays up with a plain-language error (**Couldn’t start playback**) and **Try again** / **Close** — not a toast that vanishes. On **TV**, **Try again** is focused; **↓** reaches **Close**; **OK** activates. For series, the loading screen shows the selected episode as **S02E05** (and torrent/debrid source when applicable) under the status line. While Auto checks Forja servers, the status shows steps (**Finding servers…** → **Checking servers…** → **Probing streams…** → **Opening player…**) — provider names stay in the layers server list next to **Cancel**, not in the status text
- **Cancel**, **Escape**, leaving the title, or switching to another shell tab (e.g. IPTV) while servers are still checking **stops** the search — Forja does not keep probing the next embed in the background. On **Android TV**, canceling loading returns D-pad focus to **Play** / **Resume**
- Returning to the same title/episode reuses a cached **webstreaming** extract when still reachable (see [Webstreaming](direct-streaming-mode.md)) — not Stremio Direct, torrent, or Forja auto
- White **Play** / **Resume** (link icon) opens **Sources** so you pick a torrent/Stremio/Nuvio/Forja stream. Tapping a row immediately blocks further taps (dim + spinner) until the loading screen or player takes over — so you cannot double-tap or poke the page during the short lag before resolve starts. Tapping a **Forja** HTTP row shows the cinematic loading screen while that file is checked; if it is gone (404 / unreachable), you get a toast and stay on details — it does not silently probe every other file from the same plugin
- **Trailer** (when available) opens the in-app trailer player on the best-matching official trailer
- Add or move a title on **My List** (glass **+** in the hero) — floating status menu (Plan to Watch / Watching / On Hold / Completed / Dropped), same choices as Home poster bookmarks. Click the active status again to remove it. Writes local; Simkl when logged in. **Movies:** starting playback moves the pin to Watching; finishing ≥85% moves it to Completed (reopening details with saved progress also catches up the pin)
- Trakt/Simkl/collect actions via the **⋯** overflow menu in the hero
- See resume progress in the hero for movies and the selected TV episode
- Clear resume progress with the trash icon next to **Resume** (removes that movie or selected episode from continue watching, drops the cached stream, and removes Simkl/Trakt watched history so a finished movie is no longer Completed)
- For TV: pick a season from the horizontal poster row (multi-season shows) — the hero backdrop crossfades to that season's poster; **double-click or right-click a season poster** to mark or unmark every aired episode in that season. Then tap an episode card to **select** it (does not start playback or open Sources). Click the card's **play** button to play or resume that episode (webstreaming when enabled, otherwise opens **Sources**). Hero green **Play** / **Resume** and white **Play** / **Resume** (link icon) still apply to the **selected** episode. Without Continue Watching, the page opens season 1 unless you have an in-progress episode (2–85%), then that season is selected. Hero shows series progress (`N of T · %` or **Completed**) from watched marks; episodes auto-mark at ≥85% (right-click or double-click still toggles). Marking watched also moves My List / Simkl list status: new or Plan to Watch → **Watching**; all episodes marked → **Completed**; unmarking while Completed → **Watching** (episode history still syncs to Simkl/Trakt when connected).
- Browse main cast and “More Like This” recommendations below the hero
- Search and sort torrent results in Sources (seeders, size, etc.)
- On **Android TV**, opening **Sources** moves the remote into the panel (kind tabs → provider chips → search/filters → list) — including after you leave the player and open Sources again. **Back** closes Filters if they are open, otherwise the panel — it does not leave details — and D-pad returns to **Play**. Holding **↑/↓** in the list speeds up the further you hold. Hold **OK** for 1 second on the selected Torrents / Stremio / Nuvio / Forja tab to reload that category (existing rows stay visible while providers re-check). Search highlights on D-pad focus; **OK** opens the keyboard
- Pick a provider from the chip row under the Torrents / Stremio / Nuvio / Forja tabs (Torrents: first enabled indexer loads; tap **All** to search every enabled built-in provider — only All stays highlighted; tap **All** again to clear — or from All tap one provider to filter; Stremio fetches only the addon you tap; tapping a Stremio addon keeps that chip selected even when it returned no streams — the list shows **No streams found in …** instead of silently snapping back); switching Torrents/Stremio chips mid-load **stops** the previous indexer/addon and tapping it again continues; Nuvio and Forja chips **add** to the in-flight search (up to 10 at once, 5 on TV) instead of cancelling; deselecting the last Forja/Nuvio chip **stops** that tab’s load (spinner clears) — tapping a chip again starts a fresh search; leaving the player and reopening Sources keeps your chips (opening Sources mid-playback switches to the Forja/Nuvio/… tab of the playing stream but does **not** lock chips to that one provider — **All** and multi-select stay as you left them), and Forja auto Play merges whatever loading already extracted into the same Forja list/session cache (no separate auto-only results); filter by quality, size (`<1 GB` · `1–3 GB` · `3–8 GB` · `8–20 GB` · `20 GB+`, multi-select OR), language, tech, and audio; under Nuvio, opening the tab with **All** (or any selected scrapers) keeps up to 10 in flight (5 on TV) until every selected one has been tried; tap **All** to load every scraper (only All highlighted; keeps rows you already have and only fetches the rest) — tap **All** again to clear — or from All tap one scraper to filter to it; without All, tap scrapers to multi-select (tap a fetched scraper again to deselect and clear its rows); **Settings → Sources → Nuvio → Select All by default** chooses whether every enabled scraper starts selected; **Settings → Sources → Forja plugins → Select All by default** does the same for Forja HTTP plugins (Videasy, Vidlink, Vixsrc — sniff servers stay on green Play); selection is remembered on this device; Forja **All** works the same way (only All highlighted; keeps existing plugin rows, fetches the newly selected ones; from All tap one plugin to filter) and keeps up to 10 plugins in flight (5 on TV) as slots free. Bundled Videasy lists every player.videasy.to server that answers (same Servers-tab names as green Play Videasy) and expands HLS masters into quality rows when the API label is not a resolution. Forja cards keep a clean show title / episode / year, then the same meta chips as Torrents (quality, size, container, codec, audio, HDR/source) when the plugin returns a release label, size, or file type — no emoji dump lines.
- Resolve torrents through debrid when configured
- Use [Webstreaming](direct-streaming-mode.md) via the hero green **Play** / **Resume** (play icon) button

## Tips

- On **desktop**, drag-select the stylized hero title to copy it (TMDB logo titles are images, not text). Long text titles shrink and wrap up to **3 lines** so they don’t clip into the meta row.
- Sort order for torrents is set in Settings → Sources
- Enable only **Webstreaming** under Play sources if you want a single green **Play** / **Resume** without torrent/Stremio **Sources**
