# Similar

> Find movies like ones you love using BestSimilar recommendations.

## What it is

The Similar hub lets you search TMDB or pick from trending titles, then shows a grid of similar movies powered by BestSimilar.com. Useful when you've finished something great and want the same vibe.

## How to open it

The **Similar** tab is temporarily hidden from the navigation bar and Settings → Features.

## What you can do

- Search for a movie by title
- Pick from trending suggestions
- Browse the similar-movies grid for your pick
- Open any result to view TMDB details and play

## Tips

- Similar focuses on movies — pick a film you enjoyed as the starting point
- Results link back into the normal details and playback flow
