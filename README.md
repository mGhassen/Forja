# Forja

**Your cinema universe.** One app for everything you watch, read, and listen to.

Movies, series, live TV, sports, music, manga, comics, audiobooks — no subscriptions required to the app itself. Bring your own sources, addons, and servers.

---

## Movies & TV

Browse and search with rich metadata from TMDB. Pick up where you left off with watch history and auto-resume.

- Stream from torrents, debrid, or direct links
- Stremio addon catalog support
- Real-Debrid and TorBox integration
- Jellyfin — connect your home server and watch your library
- Discover new titles, find similar picks, build your list

---

## Live & IPTV

Watch what's on right now.

- **Live Matches** — sports and events from multiple stream sources
- **IPTV** — Xtream Codes portals and M3U playlists, live channels and VOD

---

## Music

Full music player built in.

- Search and stream via Deezer metadata
- Audio playback with synced lyrics
- Playlists, likes, saved albums
- Download tracks for offline listening
- Shuffle, repeat, queue — the works

---

## Read

Manga, comics, books, and audiobooks in the same app.

- Manga and comics — page-by-page or continuous scroll, chapter tracking
- Audiobooks — chapter navigation, playback speed control
- Books library

---

## More content hubs

Dedicated sections when you want something specific:

- **Anime** · **Anime Arabic** · **Asian Drama** · **Arabic** cinema
- **Media Downloader** — grab what you want to keep offline
- **Magnet** — paste a magnet link and play

---

## Player & experience

- Unified player across desktop and mobile
- Picture-in-picture
- Dark theme
- Cross-platform: **Windows**, **Linux**, **macOS**, **Android**, **iOS**
- Auto-update check on launch
